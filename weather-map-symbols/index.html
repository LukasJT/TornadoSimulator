Radar reflectivity

The classic scale from clear air (blue) to strong storms (magenta) measures how much energy a radar beam gets back.

5-15 dBZ
Light drizzle, clear air return, dust. Not weather-significant.
20-30 dBZ
Light rain. Typical stratiform cloud precipitation.
40-50 dBZ
Heavy rain, marginal severe. Thunderstorm territory.
55-65 dBZ
Severe thunderstorm core. Likely hail.
70+ dBZ
Very large hail or lofted tornado debris (TDS).
