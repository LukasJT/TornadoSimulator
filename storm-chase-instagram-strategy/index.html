Chase Instagram strategy

Ten thousand chase photos exist for every one that gets seen. Here is how to actually build an audience if that's your goal.

The honest truth

Most chase Instagram accounts stay under 1,000 followers. A few grow to 100k+. The pattern isn't random.

Growth requires: consistent posting, distinctive style, engagement with community, patience.

What actually works

  1. Post consistently — 3-5x per week during chase season.
  2. Consistent aesthetic — signature editing style.
  3. High image quality — 4K+ resolution.
  4. Compelling captions — story or context.
  5. Location tags and specifics.
  6. Time-relevant posts (during outbreak vs 3 weeks later).
  7. Reels/short video — algorithm loves.
  8. Engage with other chasers.
  9. Cross-promote on Twitter, YouTube, TikTok.
  10. Stories daily during chase season.

The hashtag strategy

  • Mix of large and niche tags.
  • #stormchaser (medium size).
  • #tornado (very large).
  • #supercell (niche).
  • #weather (broad).
  • Location tags: #kansas #oklahoma.
  • Community tags: #skywarn #chaser.
  • Avoid banned or shadowbanned tags.
  • 5-15 tags per post.
  • First comment approach vs caption.

The posting cadence

Chase season (Mar-Jun)
3-5 posts/week + daily stories.
Peak May
5-7 posts/week + all-day stories during outbreaks.
Post-season (Jul-Sep)
2-3 posts/week — case studies, behind-the-scenes.
Off-season (Oct-Feb)
1-2 posts/week — historical shots, gear reviews.
Winter storm events
Boost posting frequency.

The engagement game

  1. Reply to every comment for first 100 followers.
  2. Comment on other chaser accounts.
  3. DM back thoughtful messages.
  4. Share other chasers' work (with credit).
  5. Join comment pods carefully.
  6. Show up in stories.
  7. Ask questions in captions.
  8. Use polls in stories.

The algorithm reality

  • Instagram rewards engagement per follower.
  • Reels reach more than photos currently.
  • Consistency beats quality alone.
  • Timing matters — post when audience active.
  • Save/share > like for algorithm.
  • Instagram punishes reposts.
  • Original content wins.

Building a distinctive style

  • Color grade consistently.
  • Recognizable frame.
  • Signature composition tendencies.
  • Consistent voice in captions.
  • Watermark discreetly.
  • Return to specific themes.
  • Followers should recognize your work without seeing the handle.

The pitfalls

  • Selling to Instagram audience but not diversifying.
  • Chasing "viral" instead of consistent quality.
  • Buying followers (algorithm punishes).
  • Filter dependency.
  • Ignoring negative feedback that's actually correct.
  • Overposting during high-stakes real events.
  • Livestreaming during personal risk.

The long game

  • Growth takes 3-5 years typically.
  • Content compounds — old posts still get discovered.
  • Consistency over years matters more than viral hits.
  • Community respect > follower count.
  • Character over algorithm.
  • Be patient.
  • Do not quit day job for social income.
