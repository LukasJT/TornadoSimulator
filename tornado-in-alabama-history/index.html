Tornadoes in Alabama

Alabama averages 47 tornadoes per year. Here is the complete history.

  • Average: 47 tornadoes/year.

Notable events

  • 1932 Deep South Outbreak impact — hundreds dead.
  • 1974 Super Outbreak impact — massive AL devastation.
  • 1998 Birmingham F5 — 32 dead.
  • 2011 April 27 Super Outbreak — 250+ dead in AL alone.
  • 2011 Tuscaloosa-Birmingham EF4 — 65 dead across region.
  • 2011 Hackleburg EF5 — 43 dead.
  • 2011 Rainsville EF5 — multiple dead.
  • 2019 March 3 Beauregard EF4 — 23 dead.
  • 2024 outbreaks — continued activity.
