Why do narrow snow bands form in a nor’easter?

Tornado Hub · November 21, 2026

Narrow nor’easter snow bands form where ascent concentrates moisture and ice growth into a strip. Frontogenesis and deformation aloft can focus that ascent on the cold side of the storm.

Advertisement

How it works

A strengthening horizontal temperature gradient can force a secondary circulation, with rising air in a favored part of the frontal zone. When that ascent overlaps temperatures and moisture favorable for snow growth, radar can show a persistent band. A band’s motion matters as much as its peak rate when estimating accumulation.
