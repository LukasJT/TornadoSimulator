Did the 2003 Presidents’ Day storm set a Baltimore snow record?

Tornado Hub · November 21, 2026

The February 2003 Presidents’ Day storm set a then-record four-day snowfall total of 28.2 inches at Baltimore/Washington International Airport, according to the contemporaneous NWS report.

Advertisement

How it works

The report identifies the intense snowfall as falling on February 16–17. It distinguishes the four-day storm accumulation at BWI from February’s monthly total of 40.5 inches. Nearby airports reported smaller four-day storm totals. The four-day BWI ranking is the one reported at the time. A current record check would have to compare later events under the same four-day definition.

Can the 40.5-inch monthly total be compared directly with a later storm total?

It includes snowfall from the full month. Compare monthly totals with monthly totals, or match storm windows before ranking individual events.
