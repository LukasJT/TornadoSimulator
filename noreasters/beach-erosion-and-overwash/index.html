What happens to beaches and dunes during a nor’easter?

Tornado Hub · November 21, 2026

Waves can erode the beach face, hit a dune, overtop it, or submerge it depending on total water level and dune elevation. These regimes leave different coastal changes.

Advertisement

How it works

The USGS storm-impact framework describes collision when wave runup reaches the dune face, overwash when runup crosses the crest, and inundation when the elevated water level itself keeps the crest submerged. A poststorm photo can show sand redistributed landward, but pre- and poststorm profiles are better for measuring how much elevation changed.
