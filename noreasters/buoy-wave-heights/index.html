How big are the waves during a nor’easter?

Tornado Hub · November 21, 2026

Nor’easter wave height varies with wind strength, fetch, duration and location. Use the relevant buoy to see what was measured as the storm passed.

Advertisement

How it works

The National Data Buoy Center reports significant wave height, a statistical height roughly comparable to the mean of the highest third of waves in the sampling period. Individual waves can be higher. Read it with dominant period, wind and timestamp. An offshore reading describes the buoy and can differ from what waves do in shallow water near the beach.

A buoy shows 5 m significant wave height. Can you conclude every wave was 5 m?

No. It summarizes the wave field. Use the sampling definition and other wave statistics to interpret extremes.
