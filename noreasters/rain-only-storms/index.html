Can a nor’easter bring only rain?

Tornado Hub · November 21, 2026

Yes. A nor’easter can bring rain throughout the event at a coastal site. Temperatures through the air column govern the precipitation type. Strong onshore wind can still raise coastal water levels.

How it works

Snowflakes falling into a deep layer above freezing can melt completely. If the remaining air and surfaces are warm enough, the drops remain liquid. Follow the lowest several kilometres of the profile and watch for cooling or a shift in winds. A site farther inland can keep a cold column under the same storm.

What should a coastal forecast include alongside rainfall?

Compare the local tide forecast with observed water levels and monitor the coastal flood product. For example, when observed water is 7.1 ft and the predicted tide is 5.8 ft at the same instant and datum, the simple observed-minus-predicted residual is 1.3 ft. Local flooding depends on the total water level and thresholds at that station. These numbers are illustrative.
