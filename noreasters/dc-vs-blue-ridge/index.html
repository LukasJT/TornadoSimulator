Why do Washington, DC and the Blue Ridge get different nor’easter totals?

Tornado Hub · November 21, 2026

Washington, DC and the Blue Ridge can see different snow totals because the region straddles important temperature, elevation and moisture gradients during coastal storms.

Advertisement

How it works

Start with the NWS Baltimore/Washington winter weather cases. Compare a city station and a mountain station for the same storm window. A higher station may be cold enough to accumulate while the valley mixes with rain. Easterly flow can rise along an exposed slope and favor precipitation. Track and band placement can still override that pattern in an individual event.

Does a higher station always receive more snow?

No. The heavier band can miss it or the slope can lie on the lee side of the wind. Compare actual observations.
