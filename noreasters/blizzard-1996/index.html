Where did the Blizzard of 1996 leave its heaviest snow?

Tornado Hub · November 21, 2026

During the Blizzard of 1996, heavy bands crossed the central Appalachians and the Mid-Atlantic. In the West Virginia sample analyzed by NWS Charleston, Elkins recorded the highest January 7 snowfall among the five stations listed.

How it works

The NWS analysis shows a strong upper trough and developing surface low on January 6. Deep moisture transport and a deformation zone concentrated snow north and west of the low on January 7. Wraparound bands persisted as the storm moved off New Jersey on January 8. Compare these five January 7 values within their shared reporting day. Full storm totals and a wider geographic ranking would require a separate dataset.

How sharp was the observed difference in this sample?

Elkins recorded 11.6 inches more than Huntington on January 7. That contrast describes these two sites during the shared reporting day.
