How did the 1991 Halloween nor’easter become the Perfect Storm?

Tornado Hub · November 21, 2026

The 1991 Halloween nor’easter became known as the Perfect Storm after a frontal low, strong high pressure to its north and moisture from Hurricane Grace contributed to a severe maritime event.

Advertisement

How it works

The NOAA Ocean Prediction Center retrospective traces a frontal low southeast of the Canadian Maritimes and an unusually strong continental high. Hurricane Grace supplied moisture to the system. The pressure contrast helped drive prolonged winds and large waves, with effects extending far along the coast. A tropical phase later developed within the broader system, distinct from the earlier frontal phase.

Why did coastal damage continue through several tides?

Winds, surge and waves persisted as tides rose and fell. Duration increased the number of times waves could reach exposed shorelines.
