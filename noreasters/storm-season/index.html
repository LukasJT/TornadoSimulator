When is nor’easter season, and can one happen in summer?

Tornado Hub · November 21, 2026

Nor’easters can occur in any month, but the National Weather Service identifies September through April as the period when they are most frequent and violent.

Advertisement

How it works

The cool-season temperature contrast between land and the western Atlantic often supports coastal cyclone development. In summer a frontal coastal storm can still develop, where its primary local impacts may be wind, rain and rough seas. Distinguish a nor’easter’s origin and structure from a tropical system that happens to bring northeasterly gusts.

Does a nor’easter have to produce snow to count?

No. The coastal storm type is defined by its location and circulation. Precipitation depends on temperature.
