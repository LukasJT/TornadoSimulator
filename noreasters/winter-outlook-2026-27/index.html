How should I read the 2026–27 winter outlook for nor’easters?

Tornado Hub · November 21, 2026

The November 19, 2026 CPC outlook favors a warmer December–February across the Northeast and above-normal total precipitation in much of the region. Those probabilities describe seasonal categories. They cannot tell you whether a nor’easter will hit a specific town.

How it works

The November 19 long-lead discussion places above-normal seasonal temperature probabilities over 50% across the Northeast and favors wetter conditions across much of the region. The precipitation category combines rain and snow as liquid equivalent. Check the map at the precise location and use the latest monthly or seasonal issuance as conditions evolve. For individual storms consult the local short-range forecast and warnings.

Could an above-normal temperature season still have a major snowstorm?

Yes. A seasonal average can be above normal while a storm occurs during a short cold interval.
