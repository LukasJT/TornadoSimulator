Will a nor’easter affect me in 2026? A local forecast walkthrough

Find the official hourly forecast and winter alerts for your town in the US or Canada, then check when wind, snow or coastal flooding could affect you.

Tornado Hub · November 21, 2026

A nor’easter can affect your town if its forecast wind, precipitation or coastal flooding reaches your location. In the United States, enter your ZIP code at weather.gov and check the point forecast and any warnings. In Canada, search your town at weather.gc.ca and open the local alert.

Check three different times

The issue time tells you when the forecast was produced. The valid interval tells you when the conditions apply. Open the separate alert to check its effective time, affected locations and expiry. If a product shows UTC, use the local offset for that date. December in Boston is EST (UTC−5), while Halifax uses AST (UTC−4).

Follow a dated example

The NWS hourly graph walkthrough shows an archived forecast values example for December 4, 2015 at 7 p.m. Follow the date across the top and hour along the bottom. On your point forecast, use the same steps to compare precipitation type and wind gusts at your intended departure and arrival times. For Halifax, select the local hourly forecast and then open the alert for the exact area you plan to travel through. An hourly forecast has a time for each value. Look on the separate warning for its expiration.

What if there is no warning for my town?

Check the local forecast and the latest statement from the issuing office. The coastal flood warning may apply to a different area than a winter storm warning. For US travel, the Winter Storm Severity Index shows modeled impact components. Check road and ferry operator updates for actual closures.
