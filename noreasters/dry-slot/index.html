What does a dry slot mean for snow in a nor’easter?

Tornado Hub · November 21, 2026

A dry slot is relatively dry air wrapping into a cyclone. It can interrupt a snow band or turn steady snow into lighter precipitation while the storm still affects the area.

Advertisement

How it works

Follow water-vapor imagery to see the dry intrusion and use radar to check where echoes diminish. Satellite water-vapor imagery samples layers aloft, while surface precipitation depends on the whole column. A rotating band on the back side can return after a lull. Look for several successive scans and surface observations before concluding accumulation has ended.

Can a dry slot end snowfall everywhere in the cyclone?

It affects particular sectors. Other parts of the storm can continue producing snow as the dry air wraps inward.
