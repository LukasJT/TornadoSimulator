What does a negative NAO mean for East Coast nor’easters?

Tornado Hub · November 21, 2026

A negative North Atlantic Oscillation generally means the usual pressure contrast across the North Atlantic is weaker. It can accompany blocking patterns that alter storm tracks, but the sign alone does not predict a nor’easter.

How it works

The CPC index describes a large-scale circulation pattern. Look at the actual ridge and trough positions for a particular event. Blocking can slow motion or redirect systems, while coastal development still requires a favorable shortwave, low-level temperature gradient and moisture. Compare the index with weather maps rather than using it as a local snowfall threshold.

Does a negative index guarantee snow in coastal cities?

No. The coastal temperature profile and track still determine whether precipitation falls as snow or rain.
