How much water is in a foot of nor’easter snow?

Tornado Hub · November 21, 2026

A foot of snow contains different amounts of water depending on its snow-to-liquid ratio. Divide snow depth by that ratio to get liquid equivalent in the same units.

Advertisement

How it works

At a 10:1 ratio, 12 inches of snow contains 1.2 inches of water. At 20:1 it contains 0.6 inches. Measuring melted snow can establish the event’s actual water equivalent. Use a representative sample and note whether it includes previously fallen snow, ice or drifts.

How much water is in 12 inches at 8:1?

12/8 = 1.5 inches of water, illustrating why wet snow can contain more water at the same depth.
