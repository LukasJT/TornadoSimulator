How can I tell if a nor’easter broke my town’s snowfall record?

Tornado Hub · November 21, 2026

Check your town’s official station, observation window and existing record before declaring a nor’easter broke it. Storm totals, calendar days and rolling 24-hour totals answer different questions.

Advertisement

How it works

Open the national or local climate record and identify the station, date and measurement definition. Compare the new report with the old record using the same station series and measurement window. For formal state records, use the responsible climate extremes committee’s decision. The February 2026 Rhode Island decision reviewed the station worksheet and site before certifying 37.9 inches over 24 hours at T. F. Green Airport.

Can you call a neighborhood total a record because it exceeds the airport’s previous daily record?
