Which nor’easter was the worst? Three ways to rank the storms

Tornado Hub · November 21, 2026

The worst nor’easter depends on what you measure. Compare station snowfall, regional snow impact and coastal flooding using separate time windows and records.

How it works

NOAA’s Regional Snowfall Index uses snowfall coverage and the affected population. A station record instead assigns a measured depth to one location and period. An observed water level belongs to a tide gauge and its vertical datum. These metrics can identify different storms. For example Rhode Island’s 37.9-inch certified 24-hour snowfall from February 2026 is a different comparison than the five-high-tide exposure of the 1962 coastal storm.
