Why do two nor’easter forecast models move the storm differently?

Tornado Hub · November 21, 2026

Forecast models can move a nor’easter differently because they initialize the atmosphere differently and approximate physical processes on different grids. Small differences in an upstream trough can grow into track differences near the coast.

How it works

Compare exactly the same valid time first. A 00 UTC run’s 24-hour prediction and a 12 UTC run’s 12-hour prediction can both verify at 00 UTC the next day. Examine the location and timing of the upper shortwave, surface low and any coastal redevelopment. A track farther offshore can move the strongest lift away from land. Ensemble spread is more informative than selecting a single forecast model because it shows multiple plausible tracks.

How can you avoid assigning a false model disagreement?

Check valid time, model cycles and positions on the same map. A storm advancing between two different valid times can appear to be a disagreement.
