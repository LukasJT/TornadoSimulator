Why do roads refreeze after the nor’easter ends?

Tornado Hub · November 21, 2026

Roads can refreeze after a nor’easter when meltwater or standing water remains and pavement temperature drops below freezing. The air temperature at a nearby station gives only part of the picture.

Advertisement

How it works

Look at the road’s surface temperature, shade, drainage and whether treatment has changed the freezing point. Bridge decks exchange heat with air both above and below. A wet deck can cool differently from pavement over soil. Check the road agency’s reports during the hours after precipitation ends as well as during the storm.

Can a dry stretch refreeze simply because pavement cools?

Refreezing requires water. A neighboring wet stretch can develop ice while a dry stretch stays clear.
