How do I read the Winter Storm Severity Index during a nor’easter?

Tornado Hub · November 21, 2026

Use the Winter Storm Severity Index to see where a forecast storm could cause disruption and which ingredient drives it. Start with the forecast’s issuance and valid window, then compare the overall category with its component maps.

How it works

The Weather Prediction Center combines components such as snow amount, snow load, ice accumulation, blowing snow, flash freeze and ground blizzard. The displayed overall category is driven by the highest component severity at a location. Click through to see whether the same category represents heavy snow on trees and lines, wind blowing snow across roads, or another modeled impact.

How should the reader use two snapshots?

To compare counties, read both at the same valid time, then check the component panel rather than inferring equal hazards from equal overall colors. For decisions about a real storm, open the local warning and road reports. The table illustrates product interpretation with synthetic counties and categories.
