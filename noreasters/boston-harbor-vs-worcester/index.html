Boston Harbor or Worcester: why can the same nor’easter feel different?

Tornado Hub · November 21, 2026

Boston Harbor and Worcester can experience different precipitation and wind in the same nor’easter. Boston is exposed to marine air and coastal water, while Worcester’s inland location and higher terrain change its temperature and wind exposure.

How it works

In the January 2022 blizzard, the Weather Prediction Center lists both Boston and Worcester among qualifying blizzard sites. Boston Logan recorded 23.8 inches during the storm. The shared classification reflects wind and visibility criteria, not equal snowfall or coastal impacts. At the harbor compare winds with water levels. Inland, compare accumulation and visibility at a representative station.

Why can Worcester remain snowy when Boston changes to rain in another storm?

Marine air can warm the lower atmosphere along the harbor while inland elevations remain colder. The actual track and wind direction determine how far that air travels.
