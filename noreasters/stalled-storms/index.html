Why can a nor’easter stall through several high tides?

Tornado Hub · November 21, 2026

A nor’easter can stall when the steering pattern moves slowly or a blocking ridge impedes progression. A slow storm can sustain the same onshore wind through multiple high tides.

How it works

Track the low against its upper-level steering flow, and compare predicted and observed coastal water levels at each peak. During the March 1962 Ash Wednesday storm, NWS Delaware describes a system lingering through five high tides. Each tide added to the opportunity for flooding while wind and waves continued. The North Atlantic Oscillation describes one aspect of the larger pattern and cannot diagnose an individual stall on its own.

Why should an impact forecast cover the next high tide after rain ends?

Onshore wind and residual water can persist after the precipitation shield moves away. Check the next predicted tide and the current gauge trend together.
