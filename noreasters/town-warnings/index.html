Where do I find an official nor’easter warning for my town?

Tornado Hub · November 21, 2026

For US towns use the National Weather Service point forecast and active alerts. In Canada use Environment and Climate Change Canada’s city forecast and weather alerts.

Advertisement

How it works

Enter the town or postal code and open the notice for the affected zone. Check its issue time, effective period, expiry, and named hazards. Coastal flooding and winter precipitation can generate separate alerts. If your route spans multiple zones, repeat the lookup for intermediate towns. A forecast graphic or news headline gives less detail than the official notice’s zone and timing.

What if the closest large city has a warning but your point forecast does not?

Check the alert’s actual area and boundaries and review your individual forecast. Nearby areas can have different alerts.

Sources

  • National Weather Service · Winter weather warnings, watches and advisories
  • Environment and Climate Change Canada · Weather alerts and local lookup
