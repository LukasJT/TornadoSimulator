Why can an Albany-area nor’easter bury the hills and spare the valley?

Tornado Hub · November 21, 2026

An Albany-area nor’easter can produce deep snow in the surrounding hills while valleys see a mix or less accumulation. Elevation and the wind direction relative to terrain both matter.

Advertisement

How it works

Check the temperature profile above the Hudson Valley and up the adjacent slopes. A valley below a marginal melting layer may receive rain or wet mix while higher terrain remains in snow. Upslope flow can enhance snowfall on windward slopes. NWS Albany’s historical storm archive provides case context, and published station totals can test the proposed mechanism for a specific date.

Could a cold valley still get less snow than nearby terrain?

Yes. Precipitation can be weaker in the valley or concentrated in a band over higher ground even when both sites stay below freezing.
