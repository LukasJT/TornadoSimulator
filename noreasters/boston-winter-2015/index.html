Why was Boston’s winter of 2015 so snowy?

Tornado Hub · November 21, 2026

Boston’s 2014–15 snowfall record came from a concentrated sequence of storms from late January through February. The season built a record through repeated events rather than a single storm.

Advertisement

How it works

The NWS Boston retrospective reports that Boston entered 2015 with 2.9 inches, accumulated 40.5 inches in the seven days from January 27 to February 2 and reached 99.4 inches for the December–February meteorological winter. Its later seasonal total reached 110.6 inches, exceeding the previous 107.6 inches from 1995–96. The seven-day interval and meteorological-winter total overlap and must never be added together.

By how much did the 2014–15 season exceed the 1995–96 total listed by NWS?

110.6−107.6 = 3.0 inches. Compare the same seasonal definition.
