Why did the January 2022 nor’easter produce a sharp snowfall cutoff in New England?

Tornado Hub · November 21, 2026

The January 2022 blizzard concentrated its heaviest snow in an eastern band. Near its inland edge, a small shift in the band changed how long neighboring areas stayed under strong lift and steady snow.

How it works

The Weather Prediction Center highlights heavy banding and storm totals up to 30 inches in several eastern Massachusetts communities. Boston Logan received 23.8 inches and Providence received 19.3 inches. Those site values document spatial variation alongside the banding analysis. To quantify the sharp inland cutoff itself, compare nearby stations on the same transect and read the storm-total map, rather than treating the most distant airport pair as the cutoff.

Why is the highest instantaneous snowfall rate insufficient to predict the storm total?

Accumulation depends on rate over time. The position and persistence of the band control how long each site receives heavy snow.
