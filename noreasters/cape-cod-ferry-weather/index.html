What should Cape Cod ferry passengers check before a nor’easter?

Tornado Hub · November 21, 2026

Before a Cape Cod ferry trip during a nor’easter, check the ferry operator’s notice for your actual sailing and the NWS marine forecast for the crossing.

Advertisement

How it works

Use the route and departure time to locate the relevant marine zone. Read forecast winds, gusts, wave period, height and hazards. A buoy tells you what was observed at its own position and last report time. The operator’s current notice determines whether a scheduled sailing is running.

What if the marine forecast looks better than an earlier notice?

Check a current operator update for the specific sailing. Vessel, crew and terminal conditions can affect the decision.
