When does a nor’easter become a ground blizzard?

Read a blizzard observation timeline and learn how blowing snow can continue after the snowfall ends.

Tornado Hub · November 21, 2026

A ground blizzard occurs when strong wind lifts loose snow from the surface and severely reduces visibility. Under US National Weather Service criteria, a blizzard can qualify with falling or blowing snow, sustained winds or frequent gusts of at least 35 mph, and prevailing visibility of one-quarter mile or less for at least three hours.

Apply the three-hour test

From 5:25 a.m. to 8:25 a.m. is three hours. The full interval in the NWS airport summary lasted 8 hours and 35 minutes. The summary documents the combined blizzard conditions at PVD. It does not identify a separate ground-blizzard-only interval.

Tell blowing snow from fresh snowfall

Compare the surface observation’s present-weather codes, visibility and gusts. Loose snow can keep blowing after falling snow ends, especially in open areas. For example, if blowing snow alone keeps visibility at one-quarter mile and gusts frequently reach 38 mph for four hours, the hypothetical conditions meet the listed US criteria. Brief gusts or a temporary drop in visibility require reading the full observation window.
