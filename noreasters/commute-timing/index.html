How do I tell when the worst part of a nor’easter will reach my commute?

Tornado Hub · November 21, 2026

Compare your departure and arrival with the hourly forecast along the whole route. The worst travel window can begin before the peak snowfall when temperatures, gusts and visibility deteriorate.

Advertisement

How it works

Open the NWS hourly graph at your start, a vulnerable checkpoint and your destination. Match their clocks and read the valid hour for precipitation type and gusts. Check the state road agency for closures and restrictions. A route crossing a higher ridge or an exposed bridge can become hazardous sooner than a neighborhood forecast suggests.

Should the heaviest snow hour define the only risky travel time?

Look for the first period when accumulation, freezing or low visibility intersects the route. That may precede the heaviest hour.
