How this simulator works

These calculations describe the environment around a hypothetical storm. Official Enhanced Fujita ratings come from surveyed damage.

Fixed-layer STP. Multiply SBCAPE / 1500, the cloud-base factor, 0–1 km SRH / 150 and the deep-layer shear factor. The cloud-base factor is (2000 − LCL) / 1000, constrained between zero and one. The shear factor is zero below 12.5 m/s, then shear / 20 up to a maximum of 1.5. Convert knots to m/s before applying the thresholds. Reference formula.

The 0–1 km energy-helicity index is CAPE × SRH / 160,000. The ideal parcel updraft speed is √(2 × CAPE). Mixing, water loading and other processes affect real updrafts. CIN provides context for initiation and is separate from this STP variant.

With 2500 J/kg CAPE, a 1000 m cloud base, 150 m²/s² helicity and 45 kt shear, the storm environment has an STP of about 1.93. Raising the cloud base to 1500 m halves the cloud-base factor and lowers the index to about 0.96.

The six ingredients

Instability CAPE
Convective available potential energy measures the positive buoyancy available to a rising air parcel.
The cap CIN
Convective inhibition measures energy that must be overcome for a parcel to reach its level of free convection.
Deep-layer shear 0–6 km
A vector difference in wind over the first six kilometers above ground.
Storm-relative helicity SRH
The low-level contribution to storm-relative helicity depends on the wind profile and storm motion.
LCL height cloud base
The lifting condensation level estimates the base of a cloud produced by lifting a surface parcel.
Surface moisture Td
Dewpoint describes moisture. This tool accepts independent CAPE and LCL inputs, so changing dewpoint leaves the calculated outputs unchanged.
