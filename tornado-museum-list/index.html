Tornado and weather museums

If you love weather, these are the pilgrimage sites. From the National Weather Museum in Norman to small regional gems.

The premier destination

National Weather Museum and Science Center
Norman, OK. Housed near NSSL. Best comprehensive weather museum.
Address
1200 W Boyd, Norman OK 73019.
Highlights
Fujita's original F5 damage assessments. VORTEX field campaign exhibits.
Visitors
Family-friendly. All ages.

For family trips

  • National Weather Museum Norman is family-perfect.
  • Cosmosphere Wichita is family-friendly.
  • Local science museum sections.
  • NWS office tours often welcoming to kids.
  • Ballpark tornado sirens (some cities have them nearby).
  • Weather balloon launches when scheduled.
  • Storm chaser events open to public.

For serious chasers

  1. Visit NSSL Norman.
  2. Attend ChaserCon.
  3. Tour SPC Norman.
  4. Visit historic outbreak sites.
  5. Read plaques and memorials.
  6. Meet local NWS forecasters.
  7. Talk to community post-event when respectful.
  8. Understand ground truth of tornadoes you've only seen on radar.

Planning a tornado history road trip

  1. Start Norman OK.
  2. North to Wichita KS.
  3. West through Woodward OK.
  4. South to Amarillo TX.
  5. East through Wichita Falls TX.
  6. Return through OKC / Moore.
  7. 7-10 day loop.
  8. Best in April-May for chase potential.
