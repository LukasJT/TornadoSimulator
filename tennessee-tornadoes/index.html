Tennessee Tornadoes

Tennessee tornado history: Nashville 2020 (25 killed), the 2011 Super Outbreak's Tennessee track, and the state's growing role as Dixie Alley's northern edge.

Notable Tennessee Tornadoes with Full Details

Nashville-Cookeville 2020

- March 3, 2020

EF3 through downtown Nashville plus EF4 in Cookeville. 25 killed. Read the full story ->

Hackleburg-Phil Campbell 2011 EF5 (TN portion)

- April 27, 2011

The Alabama EF5 crossed into southern Tennessee, causing additional damage there. Read the full story ->

Other Historic Tennessee Tornadoes

  • Middle Tennessee outbreak (April 27, 2011) - 6 tornadoes across the state
  • Middle Tennessee (February 15, 2016) - EF3 near Adamsville
  • Chattanooga tornadoes (April 12, 2020) - EF3, Easter Sunday outbreak
  • Nashville area (April 16, 1998) - F3

Key Facts About Tennessee Tornado Activity

  • Tennessee has documented tornado activity in every month of the year
  • Nocturnal tornado rate in Tennessee is ~40% - much higher than the national average
  • The state has invested heavily in tornado siren coverage since the 2020 Nashville event
