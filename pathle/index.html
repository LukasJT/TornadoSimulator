Pathle

Guess the famous tornado from its damage path. Each wrong guess reveals a new hint.

    How to play Pathle

    Pathle shows you the damage path of a famous tornado — its length, direction, and shape drawn on a stylised map. Your job is to name the tornado. You get five guesses. Start with just the path; each time you guess wrong, a new hint is revealed in this order:

    • Country — where in the world it struck
    • Rating — its Fujita or Enhanced Fujita intensity
    • Region — the state, province, or region
    • Year — when it happened

    A new Pathle puzzle appears every day. Switch to Endless mode to keep playing random tornadoes back to back. When you finish, share your score — the squares show how many hints you needed, without spoiling the answer.
