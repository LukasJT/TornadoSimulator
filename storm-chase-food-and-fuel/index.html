Chase logistics

Chase food and fuel

On a chase day, food is fuel and fuel is life. Here is the real logistics — from Braums to Casey's to the abandoned pump at Elm Creek.

The chase food philosophy

You will not stop for a proper meal. You will eat gas station food. Here are the rules:

  1. Eat before target time.
  2. Snack constantly. Blood sugar drives decisions.
  3. Hydrate constantly. Dehydration = bad calls.
  4. Avoid caffeine spikes — even feeling isn't worth the crash.
  5. Never chase hangry.
  6. Skip the fried gas station chicken. Trust me.

The fuel strategy

  1. Fill up when tank is at 1/2 in western Kansas / TX Panhandle.
  2. Fill up when at 1/4 elsewhere.
  3. Note gas station hours — rural ones close 8-10 PM.
  4. Have $50 cash for cash-only pumps.
  5. Backup fuel cans if going deep rural.
  6. Diesel? Fewer diesel pumps in rural — plan.
  7. Fuel-efficient chase vehicles save serious money.
  8. Chase can burn 20-30 gal in a 12-hr day.

The bathroom situation

Real talk: chasers pee outside constantly. It's a fact of chase life.

  • Gas station bathrooms: Casey's > Kum & Go > Love's > truck stops.
  • When target zone has no gas: pick a spot 200 yards off the road.
  • Women: SheWee, GoGirl, or similar make outdoor pees possible.
  • Camping toilet in vehicle for extreme situations.
  • Never pee in someone's field with them watching.
  • Never leave TP on ground.

The chase morning routine

  1. Wake early to review morning forecast.
  2. Coffee and continental breakfast at hotel.
  3. Pack food and drinks for the day.
  4. Fuel up before targeting.
  5. Hit the road by 10-11 AM.
  6. Lunch in target area at 12-1 PM.
  7. Storm chase 2-9 PM.
  8. Dinner in whatever town you end up in.

Hotels

  • Book night-of if possible — chase location changes daily.
  • Chase hubs: Woodward OK, Elk City OK, Salina KS, Kearney NE.
  • Airbnb sometimes cheaper for groups.
  • Motel 6, Super 8, Comfort Inn — reliable and cheap.
  • Ask about early check-in / late check-out.
  • Some chasers live in RVs during season.

When you're broke

  • Sleep in vehicle at truck stop or rest area (Walmart lots often OK for one night).
  • Eat entirely from Dollar General.
  • Fuel up in low-gas-tax states (KS, NE lower than CO).
  • Skip the chase tours.
  • Camp at state parks (backup for hotel).

The chase snack meta-game

  • Peanut M&Ms are chase food.
  • Beef jerky in variety pack.
  • Slim Jims for the truly gross moments.
  • Sunflower seeds keep you awake.
  • Vernors ginger ale for storm-day nausea.
  • Chocolate melts in a hot car — skip it in July.
  • Water > everything else.
