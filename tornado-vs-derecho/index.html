Tornado vs. Derecho

Tornado vs derecho: a tornado is a small vortex from a supercell. A derecho is a widespread straight-line windstorm from a squall line, often 250+ miles long with 75+ mph winds. Full comparison.

Definitions

  • Tornado: A violently rotating column of air extending from a thunderstorm to the ground, typically 100 yards to 1 mile wide, lasting minutes to hours.
  • Derecho: A long-lived, widespread band of straight-line winds produced by a squall line of thunderstorms. Officially: sustained damaging winds along a track at least 250 miles long, with wind gusts of 58+ mph, over at least a 6-hour period.

How to Tell Them Apart After the Fact

Look at Debris Pattern

The single best clue after a disaster is how debris is oriented:

  • Tornado damage: Debris scattered in multiple directions. Trees fallen in different orientations. Damage swath narrow (yards to a mile wide).
  • Derecho damage: All debris and fallen trees pointing in the same direction. Damage swath wide (miles wide).

Look at the Track Length

A single tornado rarely exceeds 100 miles of continuous damage. A derecho typically produces damage over 250-1,000 miles.

Weather Radar

Tornado supercells show hook echoes and mesocyclone signatures. Derechos appear as long bow echoes stretching across radar.

Famous US Derechos

  • June 29, 2012 Mid-Atlantic Derecho - 700-mile path, 22 killed, millions without power
  • August 10, 2020 Midwest Derecho - 770-mile path, $11 billion damage in Iowa alone, catastrophic corn crop damage
  • May 8, 2009 "Super Derecho" - traveled 1,000+ miles from Kansas to Kentucky
  • May 30-31, 1998 Corn Belt Derecho - major damage across Iowa, Illinois, Indiana

Which Is More Dangerous?

Individually, a violent tornado is far more dangerous than a derecho. Peak tornado winds exceed peak derecho winds by 2-3x. Tornadoes can lift and throw vehicles; derechos generally cannot.

But derechos affect much larger areas simultaneously - a single derecho may damage hundreds of thousands of homes across a multi-state region. Total derecho fatalities can rival small tornado outbreaks.

Can a Derecho Contain Tornadoes?

Yes. Derechos can spawn embedded tornadoes, especially at the leading edge. These tornadoes are typically brief (EF0-EF1) but can be strong. The 2012 Mid-Atlantic derecho produced multiple embedded tornadoes.

Conversely: a tornado outbreak day can also produce a derecho along the same squall line, especially in the evening as the storms consolidate.

Warnings

  • Tornado Warning - specific tornado has been detected or reported
  • Severe Thunderstorm Warning - what you get during derechos (not a specific "Derecho Warning" exists)
  • PDS Severe Thunderstorm Warning - enhanced language when derecho-level winds are expected

The NWS added enhanced severe thunderstorm warnings in 2021 specifically to raise urgency around derecho events after several were under-warned.

Safety - Similar but Not Identical

For both tornadoes and derechos, the safety rule is the same: get into a well-built structure, interior room, lowest floor. But derechos have some unique risks:

  • Long-lasting extreme winds vs. brief tornado impact
  • Widespread trees down / power outages / infrastructure damage
  • Multi-state impact means aid response is slower
