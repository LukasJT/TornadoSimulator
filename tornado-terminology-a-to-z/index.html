Tornado terminology A to Z

Every tornado term you've heard, plus dozens you haven't. From "anticyclonic" to "wedge tornado" — the definitive alphabetical reference.

A

  • Anticyclonic — rotating clockwise (opposite of typical Northern Hemisphere tornadoes).
  • Anvil — flat spreading top of mature thunderstorm.
  • Anvil crawler — lightning discharge along the anvil underside.
  • ASOS — Automated Surface Observing System. Airport weather stations.
  • Atmosphere — the layer of gas surrounding Earth.

B

  • Backing wind — wind that turns counterclockwise with height.
  • Barometer — instrument measuring pressure.
  • Beaver tail — inflow band extending from wall cloud toward rain.
  • Book-end vortex — rotation at the ends of a bow echo.
  • Bow echo — bow-shaped line of thunderstorms on radar.
  • Bulk shear — magnitude of wind change with height.

C

  • CAPE — Convective Available Potential Energy. Measure of instability.
  • Cap — warm layer aloft inhibiting convection.
  • Cirrus — high wispy ice cloud.
  • CIN — Convective Inhibition. Energy needed to overcome cap.
  • Cirrostratus — high thin cloud sheet.
  • Clear slot — RFD-driven clearing on the west/south side of a mesocyclone.
  • Convection — vertical motion of air.
  • Cumulonimbus — thunderstorm cloud.
  • Cyclonic — rotating counterclockwise (Northern Hemisphere typical).

D

  • Debris cloud — cloud of ground material lifted by a tornado.
  • Debris signature (TDS) — radar signature of tornado-lofted debris.
  • Derecho — long-lived widespread damaging wind event.
  • Dewpoint — temperature at which air is saturated.
  • Doppler — radar measuring velocity via frequency shift.
  • Downburst — concentrated downdraft producing damaging surface winds.
  • Downdraft — descending air current in a storm.
  • Dryline — boundary between moist and dry air.
  • Dust devil — small vortex from surface heating (not a tornado).

E

  • EF Scale — Enhanced Fujita Scale. Wind damage rating from EF0 to EF5.
  • Elevated storm — storm where updraft is above the surface layer.
  • Ensemble — multiple forecast runs to quantify uncertainty.
  • Enhanced Skywarn — advanced spotter training.

F

  • Fair-weather waterspout — non-supercell waterspout.
  • Fine line — radar signature of gust front.
  • Flash flood — sudden intense localized flooding.
  • Flanking line — line of towering cumulus behind a supercell.
  • Fujita scale — original (F0-F5) tornado rating, replaced by EF.
  • Funnel cloud — rotating condensation cloud NOT touching ground.

G

  • Gate-to-gate shear — velocity difference between adjacent radar gates.
  • Gust front — leading edge of storm outflow.
  • Gustnado — non-tornado spin-up along gust front.
  • GFS — Global Forecast System model.
  • GEFS — Global Ensemble Forecast System.

H

  • Hail — precipitation of ice pellets.
  • Hail core — center of highest hail concentration.
  • High Precipitation (HP) supercell — supercell with heavy rain wrapping around updraft.
  • Hodograph — plot of wind vector with height.
  • Hook echo — radar signature of mesocyclone.
  • HRRR — High-Resolution Rapid Refresh model.

I

  • Impact-Based Warning — tiered warning system (CONSIDERABLE, CATASTROPHIC).
  • Inflow — air flowing into a storm.
  • Inflow tail — cloud band pointing into storm inflow.
  • Inversion — layer where temperature increases with height.

J-K

  • Jet streak — localized zone of maximum jet stream wind.
  • Kelvin-Helmholtz waves — breaking wave cloud pattern.

L

  • Landspout — non-supercell tornado.
  • LCL — Lifted Condensation Level. Height at which parcel becomes saturated.
  • Left-mover — supercell with anticyclonic rotation moving left of mean wind.
  • Lightning — atmospheric electrical discharge.
  • Low Precipitation (LP) supercell — supercell with little precipitation.

M

  • Mammatus — pouches hanging from cumulonimbus underside.
  • Mesocyclone — rotating updraft within supercell.
  • Mesoscale Convective System (MCS) — organized cluster of storms.
  • Microburst — small-scale intense downburst.
  • Multi-vortex tornado — tornado containing multiple subvortices.
  • MUCAPE — Most Unstable CAPE.

N

  • NEXRAD — Next Generation Radar. NWS national radar network.
  • Non-supercell tornado — tornado formed by mechanism other than supercell mesocyclone.
  • Nose — leading edge of dryline.

O

  • Occlusion — mesocyclone wrapped by cool RFD.
  • Outflow — cold air spreading from storm.
  • Overshooting top — dome briefly above anvil.

P

  • PDS — Particularly Dangerous Situation. Tag on SPC outlooks.
  • PIREP — pilot report.
  • Pulse severe storm — single-cell storm producing brief severe weather.
  • PyroCB — pyrocumulonimbus. Fire-generated thunderstorm.

Q-R

  • QLCS — Quasi-Linear Convective System. Squall line.
  • Rear-Flank Downdraft (RFD) — downdraft behind updraft.
  • Rear-Inflow Jet — upper-level winds punching into back of bow.
  • Right-mover — supercell moving right of mean wind.
  • Rope out — tornado dissipating into thin rope-like funnel.

S

  • SBCAPE — Surface-Based CAPE.
  • Severe thunderstorm — storm with 1"+ hail, 58+ mph winds, or tornado.
  • Shear — change in wind with height or distance.
  • Skywarn — NWS storm spotter network.
  • Slight risk — SPC lowest severe risk category.
  • SPC — Storm Prediction Center.
  • Squall line — line of thunderstorms.
  • Storm relative helicity (SRH) — measure of streamwise rotation.
  • Subvortex — smaller vortex within multi-vortex tornado.
  • Suction vortex — synonym for subvortex.
  • Supercell — thunderstorm with persistent rotating updraft.

T

  • TDS — Tornado Debris Signature.
  • Tornado — rotating column of air in contact with ground and cloud.
  • Tornado Emergency — highest impact-based warning tag.
  • Tornado warning — imminent tornado threat.
  • Tornado watch — conditions favorable for tornadoes.
  • Training storms — successive storms over same area.
  • Tropopause — boundary between troposphere and stratosphere.
  • TVS — Tornado Vortex Signature.

U

  • Updraft — rising air current in a storm.
  • Undulatus asperitas — wavy chaotic cloud base.

V

  • Veering wind — wind that turns clockwise with height (favors tornadoes).
  • Virga — precipitation evaporating before ground.
  • VORTEX — Verification of the Origins of Rotation in Tornadoes Experiment.
  • Vorticity — measure of rotation in fluid.

W

  • Wall cloud — lowered rotating cloud below storm base.
  • Warning — imminent hazardous weather.
  • Watch — conditions favorable for hazardous weather.
  • Waterspout — tornado over water.
  • Wedge tornado — tornado wider than it is tall.
  • WEA — Wireless Emergency Alert.
  • WFO — Weather Forecast Office.
  • Whirlwind — generic term for any rotating column.

X-Y-Z

  • X-band radar — high-frequency mobile radar used by researchers.
  • Zonal flow — largely west-to-east jet stream flow.
  • Z-R relationship — reflectivity to rainfall rate conversion.
