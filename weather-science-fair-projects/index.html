Weather Science Fair Projects: Testable Questions About Rain, Wind, Heat, Clouds, and Forecasts

Weather science fair project ideas with testable questions about temperature, surfaces, rain, wind, clouds, pressure, and forecast accuracy.

Quick answer: A strong weather science fair project asks a measurable question and compares observations fairly over time.

Question ideas

Which surface heats fastest, how accurate was the forecast, how does cloud cover affect temperature, or where is wind strongest around the school?

Each question needs consistent measurements.

Variables

Control time of day, location, instrument, and method as much as possible.

Weather is noisy, so repeated observations help.

Presentation

Use charts, photos, maps, and a short explanation of errors or limitations.

Judges like clear methods.
