Watch vs Warning
👀 Watch

Tornado WATCH

Conditions are favorable. Watch out.

Bring outdoor items in. Charge phones. Know where you'd shelter.
🌪️ Warning

Tornado WARNING

Tornado sighted or radar-indicated.

SHELTER NOW. Cover your head.
Where to shelter

DO

  • Go to the basement or storm shelter
  • Interior room on lowest floor (bathroom, closet)
  • Get under sturdy furniture
  • Cover your head and neck with arms, helmet or blanket
  • Bring pets and emergency kit

DON'T

  • Don't open windows to "equalize pressure"
  • Don't shelter under overpasses
  • Don't try to outrun a tornado in a car
  • Don't stay in a mobile home
  • Don't go outside until the warning is cancelled

How to receive alerts

📻 NOAA weather radio — will wake you up at night

📱 Wireless Emergency Alerts — on by default on most phones

📺 Local TV and radio — broadcast interruptions

📢 Sirens — outdoor use only, don't rely on indoors
