🏆 World records

Every tornado record

Widest, fastest, deadliest, longest — the extremes of the tornado record book.

💨

Highest wind speed

Mobile Doppler radar measured the 1999 Bridge Creek–Moore, Oklahoma F5. Still the highest wind speed ever recorded on Earth.

301 mph1999
📏

Widest path

The 2013 El Reno, Oklahoma tornado. Rated EF3 but its width was the world record.

2.6 miles2013
🛤️

Longest continuous track

The 1925 Tri-State Tornado traveled across Missouri, Illinois and Indiana.

219 miles1925
⏱️

Longest duration (single tornado)

Also the Tri-State — approximately 3 hours 30 minutes continuously on the ground.

3h 30m1925
💀

Deadliest single tornado (US)

Tri-State again. Killed 695 people, injured 2,027, destroyed cities including Murphysboro, De Soto and Griffin.

695 dead1925
🌍

Deadliest tornado (world)

The 1989 Daulatpur–Saturia tornado in Bangladesh — worst-ever tornado disaster.

~1,300 dead1989
💰

Costliest tornado

The 2011 Joplin, Missouri EF5 — $3.6 billion in insured damages ($4.8B in 2024 dollars).

$3.6B2011
📊

Most tornadoes in one day

April 27, 2011 — the peak day of the 2011 Super Outbreak. Also the deadliest single day since 1925 (316 fatalities).

2162011
🌀

Most tornadoes in a 3-day outbreak

The 2011 Super Outbreak, April 25–28.

3602011
📅

Most tornadoes in a US calendar year

2004 — a strong El Niño year.

1,8172004
🚄

Fastest forward speed

The 1925 Tri-State averaged 62 mph forward speed. Individual segments moved as fast as 73 mph.

73 mph1925
📉

Deepest measured pressure drop

A 100 mb drop inside the 2003 Manchester, SD F4, measured by a chaser probe left in its path.

100 mb2003
📚

First officially confirmed F5 outside US

The Elie, Manitoba tornado of June 22, 2007. Still the only one.

Elie2007
🌡️

Coldest tornado

An F3 tornado near Winnetoon, NE on February 1, 1893 formed during freezing conditions. Winter tornadoes are rare but real.

Feb 18931893
🏔️

Highest-elevation tornado

The 1987 Teton-Yellowstone tornado tracked at up to 10,500 feet in elevation across Wyoming.

10,500 ft1987
🏙️

Downtown big-city tornado

The 2000 Fort Worth F3 struck downtown Fort Worth, killing 5. The 2019 Dallas EF3 damaged 100+ homes and businesses.

Fort Worth2000
🏘️

Most-hit US city

Moore, Oklahoma has been struck by two F5/EF5 tornadoes since 1999 plus half a dozen weaker events. See Moore tornado history.

Moore, OK1999-2013
📈

Longest debris path

Debris from the 2011 Tuscaloosa-Birmingham EF4 was found up to 220 miles away.

220 miles2011
🌊

Longest waterspout track

The 1900 Cottage City waterspout off Massachusetts had a documented track of about 4 miles.

4 miles1900
🎥

Most photographed tornado

Debated. The 1996 Manchester-Dimmitt TX, the 2004 Manchester SD, and the 2019 Lawrence KS tornadoes are all leading candidates. Modern cell phones make comparison impossible.

—unknown
