2026 tornado chase season

What to expect for the 2026 US tornado season. Climate signals, past patterns, and what chasers are watching.

The ENSO state

  • Late 2025 into 2026: neutral to weak La Niña conditions.
  • Historical pattern: neutral years produce variable tornado activity.
  • La Niña years often shift activity slightly eastward.
  • Strong El Niño typically means fewer Atlantic hurricanes but complicated tornado outcomes.
  • Actual season depends on many factors beyond ENSO.

The 2011 comparison year

  • Look for similar setups.
  • 2011 Super Outbreak occurred in La Niña.
  • Not deterministic but pattern relevant.
  • Modern forecasting increasingly integrates.
  • Historical analog matching.

The specific things to watch

  1. Gulf of Mexico SSTs — controlling moisture.
  2. Jet stream position — controlling steering.
  3. Snow cover extent — affects cold air source.
  4. Drought progression — affects surface energy.
  5. Model consensus 5-7 days out.
  6. Regional pattern establishment.
  7. Historical analog check.

SPC outlook interpretation

  • SPC Day 8 outlook — probabilistic.
  • Convective Outlook Day 1-3 daily.
  • Watch for pattern recognition of severe episodes.
  • April active period historically.
  • May outbreak potential.
  • Cyclic patterns of storm complexes.
  • Multi-day setups.
