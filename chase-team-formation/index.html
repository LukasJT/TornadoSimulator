Chase organization

Chase team formation

Serious chase groups need structure. Here is how to form and run a chase team.

The formation reasons

  • Shared costs.
  • Split forecasting.
  • Redundant safety.
  • Continuous documentation.
  • Community and mental support.
  • Business efficiency.
  • Skill diversity.
  • Community reputation.

The specific roles

Driver
Primary vehicle operator. Focuses on driving.
Navigator
GPS, maps, storm positioning.
Forecaster
Model consultation, target refinement.
Photographer
Cameras and imaging.
Videographer
Recording and livestream.
Communicator
Ham radio, Zello, chase group communication.
Meteorologist/analyst
Real-time weather interpretation.
Safety coordinator
Escape routes, decisions.
Team lead
Overall decisions.

The team agreement

  1. Cost splitting formula.
  2. Vehicle use and maintenance.
  3. Gear ownership and sharing.
  4. Photo/video rights.
  5. Business income splits.
  6. Communication protocols.
  7. Safety policies.
  8. Off-season commitments.
  9. Dispute resolution.
  10. Team exit provisions.

Team dynamics

  • Clear roles reduce conflict.
  • Regular team meetings.
  • Post-chase debriefs.
  • Handle disagreements privately.
  • Respect specialization.
  • Celebrate wins together.
  • Support after bust days.
  • Address concerns early.
  • Trust matters more than skill.

The post-2013 El Reno impact

  • Chase group leader responsibility.
  • Legal duty of care emphasized.
  • Safety protocols formalized.
  • Insurance considerations.
  • Team member screening.
  • Emergency response planning.
  • Communication protocols.
  • Team leadership standards.
  • Ongoing training.
  • Community accountability.

Starting a team

  1. Identify potential members.
  2. Discuss compatibility.
  3. Discuss goals and expectations.
  4. Trial chases before commitment.
  5. Draft written agreement.
  6. Attorney review if commercial.
  7. Legal formation if commercial.
  8. Insurance verification.
  9. Regular meetings.
  10. Continued team development.

Ending a team

  • Discuss openly.
  • Written agreement should have exit provisions.
  • Divide assets equitably.
  • Complete outstanding business.
  • Preserve community connections.
  • Debrief lessons learned.
  • Personal support.
  • Move forward.
  • Sometimes better as chase group.
  • Sometimes better solo.
