Barometer School Project: Track Air Pressure Before Weather Changes

A student barometer project explaining air pressure, pressure trends, storms, fair weather, and simple observation logs.

Quick answer: A barometer project shows that air pressure rises and falls as weather patterns change.

What to track

Record pressure trend, clouds, wind, temperature, and precipitation each day.

The trend is often more useful than one reading.

Weather meaning

Falling pressure can suggest an approaching disturbance, while rising pressure can suggest improving or changing conditions.

Pressure alone does not predict every storm.

Project notes

Compare your readings with a local weather station.

Explain instrument limits in the final report.
