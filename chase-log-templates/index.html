Chase log templates

Chasers who log grow. Chasers who don't plateau. Here is what to record and how.

Why log

  • Compare forecast with reality.
  • Identify pattern recognition improvements.
  • Document targets that worked or didn't.
  • Track chase decisions.
  • Learn from mistakes.
  • Build institutional knowledge.
  • Reference for future similar setups.
  • Insurance and legal documentation.
  • Community sharing potential.
  • Long-term career records.

Daily chase log elements

  1. Date.
  2. Location (start point, target zone, chase locations).
  3. Distance driven.
  4. Fuel used.
  5. Weather forecast (which model, what timing, what accuracy).
  6. Actual weather (times, locations, features observed).
  7. Chase decisions and rationale.
  8. Communication (chase partners, home base).
  9. Photographs taken.
  10. Reports made to NWS.
  11. Lessons learned.
  12. Reflections.
