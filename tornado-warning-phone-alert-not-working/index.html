Why Didn’t My Phone Get the Tornado Warning?

Common reasons a phone misses a tornado warning and how to build a more reliable severe-weather alert setup.

Quick answer
A phone may miss an alert because emergency alerts are disabled, the device is outside the targeted area, coverage is interrupted, the warning type is not sent through that channel, or app permissions are limited. Use more than one warning source.
On this pageCheck the phone firstTargeting is imperfectCreate a backup chain

Check the phone first

Review Wireless Emergency Alert settings, emergency alert volume behavior, location permissions for weather apps, notification permissions, battery optimization, and focus modes. Names differ by phone and carrier.

Update the operating system and app, then use built-in test options if available. Do not wait for severe weather to learn that an alert is silent.

Targeting is imperfect

Wireless Emergency Alerts are distributed to compatible devices connected to participating cell sites in a targeted area. Warning polygons and cellular broadcast areas do not line up perfectly, and a device may connect outside the target.

Weather apps may use a saved location, current GPS position, county subscription, or their own filtering rules. Check which one the app uses.

Create a backup chain

Add a NOAA Weather Radio with battery backup, trusted local broadcast coverage, and another official or reputable app. Make sure more than one device can wake the household at night.

If a warning is visible on an official map and includes your location, shelter even if your phone never sounded. Report persistent delivery problems to the app provider or carrier after the threat passes.

If a warning includes you

  1. Move to the best available shelter immediately.
  2. Get low and protect your head and neck from debris.
  3. Keep monitoring official updates from shelter.

Frequently asked questions

Does airplane mode receive emergency alerts?

Usually cellular broadcasts require connection to a participating cellular network; behavior varies. Do not rely on a device in airplane mode.

Why did someone beside me get the alert?

Phones can use different carriers, towers, settings, operating systems, and timing. Targeted delivery is not identical for every device.

Are weather apps official warnings?

Many redistribute NWS products, but notification rules and delays vary. Compare with weather.gov and local officials.
