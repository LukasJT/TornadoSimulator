Tornadoes in movies — ranked by realism

Stars are for accuracy, not entertainment. A campy movie can be 1★ and still fantastic; a serious drama can be 4★ and boring. This ranks the science, not the film.

Twisters
2024

The Lee Isaac Chung sequel. Real chasers and NWS meteorologists consulted; the storm structures on screen were built from real radar loops. The wedge tornado in Norman looks correct — cone, tilted updraft, RFD wrap-in. The problem: the "PARK" tornado-taming plan is pure fiction. Real tornadoes cannot be de-energized by throwing something at them.

Verdict: Structure and radar realistic, science fiction. ★★★★
Twister
1996

The Jan de Bont classic. Made real chasers a thing in popular culture. The F5 tornado at the end is well-shot but at least three tornadoes' worth of debris behavior is compressed into one. Cows do fly (see our FAQ), but not that many. Dorothy the sensor probe was inspired by real research (TOTO), but never worked as depicted.

Verdict: Foundational, imperfect. ★★★
The Wizard of Oz
1939

Made with a 35-foot muslin windsock and a compressed-air stage rig. As a tornado depiction, the shape is right — a classic cone — but the color is far too dark and the fly-away-with-a-house effect is not physically possible.

Verdict: Iconic silhouette, impossible physics. ★★
Into the Storm
2014

A found-footage tornado outbreak thriller. The F5 at the end shows a bunch of unrelated tornado behaviors mashed together — fire tornado, waterspout, EF5 wedge, multi-vortex. Real tornadoes don't do all these at once, but the effects budget went to good use on the wedge.

Verdict: Kitchen-sink tornadoes. ★★
Night of the Twisters
1996 (TV)

Based on the 1980 Grand Island, NE outbreak, itself a real event. The TV movie stays close to the actual chronology — multiple nighttime tornadoes, family separations, prolonged sheltering — and captures the chaos of a real outbreak better than most Hollywood films.

Verdict: Real event, faithful adaptation. ★★★★
Sharknado
2013

A tornado picks sharks out of the ocean and drops them on Los Angeles. Waterspouts don't lift large marine animals; they lift small water droplets and maybe small fish. Sharks would suffocate almost immediately in air. Los Angeles is not a tornado alley. The film knows exactly what it is.

Verdict: Delightful nonsense. ☆
Man of Steel
2013

Kevin Costner's Jonathan Kent dies saving the family dog from a highway tornado. Storm structure looks reasonable in wide shots. The pass over the highway is credible-ish, though the freeway would not be safe with a tornado a mile away.

Verdict: Better than most superhero tornadoes. ★★★
The Day After Tomorrow
2004

Multiple simultaneous tornadoes destroy Los Angeles. Roland Emmerich stuffs every extreme weather event into 24 hours. The Sunset Strip tornado shot is genuinely gorgeous CGI, but the atmospheric setup that produces it is nonsense.

Verdict: Visually impressive, meteorologically absurd. ★★
Storm Chasers (TV series)
2007-2012

Discovery Channel. Real chasers, real chases, real footage. Some artificial drama in the editing but the storms are absolutely real — including some of the best-photographed events of the decade.

Verdict: Non-fiction reference. ★★★★★
Tornado Alley (IMAX documentary)
2011

Sean Casey's decade of chasing captured on IMAX. Some of the best real tornado footage ever recorded. Includes the 2004 Manchester, SD tornado and multiple 2010 Plains events.

Verdict: Actual reference footage. ★★★★★
Category 6: Day of Destruction
2004 (TV)

Multiple F5 tornadoes hit Chicago during a heatwave. The made-up "Category 6" is technically nonsense (the EF scale caps at 5), but the tornado effects are decent for TV.

Verdict: Fun trash. ★★
Wind (Twister prequel graphic novel)
2024

Companion piece to Twisters (2024). Interesting for its explanation of TOTO and DOW research context.

Verdict: Serious background material. ★★★★
Iron Sky: The Coming Race
2019

Multiple tornadoes appear as briefly-glimpsed disasters in the film's chaotic third act. Barely count.

Verdict: Blink and miss. ★
Wonka
2023

A blizzard, not a tornado — but the extreme wind sequences are Timothée Chalamet territory more than meteorology.

Verdict: Not really a tornado movie. —
Twisters vs. Twister
Bonus round

Which is more accurate? Twisters (2024) has better structure and radar, thanks to consulting real meteorologists. Twister (1996) has better chase dynamics — the chase feel, the road strategy, the community of chasers. Neither depicts a real tornado end-to-end faithfully.

Verdict: Twisters wins on physics, Twister wins on culture.
