The 2016 Yancheng Tornado

On 23 June 2016, an EF4 tornado struck Funing County near Yancheng in Jiangsu Province, killing nearly a hundred people — China's deadliest tornado in roughly half a century.

At a glance

Date23 June 2016
LocationFuning County, Yancheng, Jiangsu Province
RatingEF4
ImpactNearly 100 killed, hundreds injured

What happened

On the afternoon of 23 June 2016, a violent tornado — later rated EF4 — tore through rural Funing County near the city of Yancheng in Jiangsu Province, eastern China. Striking amid heavy thunderstorms and hail, it destroyed homes and factories, downed power lines, and killed nearly a hundred people, injuring hundreds more.

Why it was so deadly

The tornado hit a densely populated agricultural region where many buildings were not built to withstand extreme wind, and where tornado warnings had little lead time. The combination of high intensity, vulnerable structures, and a dense rural population produced a catastrophic death toll — the worst from a single tornado in China in around fifty years.

Aftermath and lessons

The disaster prompted national mourning and a major relief and rebuilding effort, and it sharpened attention on tornado detection, warning systems, and building standards in China. It remains the reference point for tornado risk in the country — see the wider picture in tornadoes in China.
