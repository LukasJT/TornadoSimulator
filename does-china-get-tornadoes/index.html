Does China Get Tornadoes?

Yes — China gets tornadoes regularly, mostly on the eastern plains. Most are weak, but some have been violent and deadly. Here is where and when China's tornadoes strike.

At a glance

Short answerYes — dozens per year
Most inEastern China (Jiangsu, Anhui, Guangdong, NE)
Peak seasonSpring and summer
Deadliest2016 Yancheng, Jiangsu (EF4)

How common are Chinese tornadoes?

China records tens of tornadoes in a typical year, the great majority of them weak. They are far less frequent than in the United States, but China's enormous population means even a modest tornado can affect many people.

Where do they happen?

The main hotspots are the flat, humid plains of eastern China: the lower Yangtze region (Jiangsu, Anhui, Shanghai), the Pearl River Delta in Guangdong, and the north-eastern provinces. Coastal areas also see waterspouts.

Are they dangerous?

Usually not — but occasionally, yes, very. The 23 June 2016 Yancheng tornado in Jiangsu reached EF4 intensity and killed nearly a hundred people, the deadliest in China in decades. More on the wider pattern in tornadoes in China.
