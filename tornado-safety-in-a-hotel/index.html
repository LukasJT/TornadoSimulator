Tornado Safety in a Hotel: What to Ask and Where to Go

How to find tornado shelter in a hotel, what rooms to avoid, and how travelers can prepare before severe weather arrives.

Quick answer
Ask the front desk for the hotel’s tornado procedure before storms arrive. During a warning, follow staff to the designated shelter or use a small interior room or corridor on the lowest practical floor, away from windows and wide-span rooms.

Ask before the warning

Hotels vary in construction and layout. Identify enclosed stairs, interior corridors, restrooms, or designated shelter areas and learn whether elevators will operate during an emergency.

Turn on local alerts rather than relying on a home location saved in an app. Note the county and town because broadcasts may use both.

Choose structure over convenience

Move away from exterior glass, atriums, ballrooms, conference halls, pools, and other wide-span spaces. An interior stairwell or compact service area may offer more surrounding structure, but follow posted plans and staff directions.

Do not shelter in the car or under the entrance canopy. Bring shoes, room key, phone, and necessary medication if immediately available.

Plan for access needs

Tell the front desk ahead of time if you need mobility, hearing, vision, language, or medical assistance. A power outage can disable elevators and electronic room locks.

Keep a flashlight and backup battery ready. Afterward, avoid damaged glass, flooded areas, and elevators until staff confirms they are safe.

If a warning includes you

  1. Move to the best available shelter immediately.
  2. Get low and protect your head and neck from debris.
  3. Keep monitoring official updates from shelter.

Frequently asked questions

Should I stay in the bathtub?

Only if the bathroom is an interior, windowless, low-floor option and staff has not directed you to a better designated shelter.

Is a hotel hallway safe?

An interior corridor can be a reasonable available option when it is away from glass and large roof spans, but building-specific plans take priority.

Should I go to the parking garage?

Do not improvise a route through exposed areas during a warning. Ask hotel staff about designated shelter locations in advance.
