Tornadoes in Manitoba

Manitoba averages 8-12 tornadoes per year. Here is the complete history.

The stats

  • Average: 8-12 tornadoes/year.
  • Peak season: June-August.
  • Regional patterns.
  • Community awareness.
  • Skywarn or equivalent spotter network active.

Notable events

  • 1922 Portage la Prairie F4 — 5 dead.
  • 2007 Elie F5 — the ONLY F5 in Canadian history. 300+ mph winds estimated.
  • Multiple southern Manitoba events — annual.
  • 2024 outbreak events — continued activity.
