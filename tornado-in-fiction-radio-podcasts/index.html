For chase commutes

Long chase days need audio. Recommendations for actual chase drives:

Advertisement
  1. Local weather brief from AccuWeather app.
  2. SPC podcast if available.
  3. Longer chase podcasts for background between targets.
  4. Music playlist that doesn't distract from radar audio.
  5. Ham radio net for local reports.
  6. Weather radio SAME-programmed.
  7. AM radio for local news of storms.
