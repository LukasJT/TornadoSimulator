Severe Weather Classroom Activities: Maps, Radar, Clouds, and Safety Scenarios

Hands-on classroom activities for teaching severe weather with radar loops, cloud photos, safety decisions, alert cards, and weather map symbols.

Quick answer: Good severe weather classroom activities combine observation, vocabulary, and decision-making instead of only memorizing storm facts.

Radar and maps

Give students still radar images or loops and ask them to identify movement, heavy precipitation, and warning areas.

Pair radar with surface maps so students see how fronts and boundaries fit the storm picture.

Cloud and hazard sorting

Use cloud photos and hazard cards to sort lightning, hail, wind, flooding, winter weather, and heat risks.

The goal is not perfect forecasting. It is recognizing what information should trigger safer choices.

Scenario practice

Ask what students would do at recess, on a bus, in an apartment, or at a sports field when an alert arrives.

Short scenarios build calm, repeatable actions.
