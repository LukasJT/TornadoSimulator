Rural Storm Preparedness Guide: Long Driveways, Wells, Livestock, Roads, and Power Outages

Storm preparedness for rural homes, farms, and properties, including wells, generators, livestock, long driveways, gravel roads, and sparse alerts.

Quick answer: Rural storm preparedness needs extra attention to access, backup power, water, animals, roads, and communication gaps.

Access issues

Long driveways, gravel roads, fallen trees, and washed-out culverts can delay help.

Keep tools and supplies ready before storms.

Water and power

Private wells may not work without electricity, so store water and plan safe generator use.

Fuel supply and carbon monoxide safety are critical.

Animals and land

Plan for livestock shelter, fencing damage, feed, and debris.

Check alerts with more than one method because coverage can be uneven.
