Read the parcel definition first

The National Weather Service training deck displays the observed OUN profile for 17:00 UTC on May 20, 2013. It reports mixed-layer CAPE above 3000 J/kg and shows 72°F at the surface dewpoint trace. Verify the report on NWS slide 46.

Repeat the limited transfer

Compare default 68°F with the cited 72°F dewpoint. The calculated outputs stay the same because the lab takes CAPE and cloud-base height as independent inputs. The six slider values omit the pressure, temperature, dewpoint and wind profiles by level, including the shape of the buoyancy area. This comparison carries over one observed value and leaves the rest as synthetic controls.
