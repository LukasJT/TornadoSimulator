Tornadoes in Michigan by the numbers

The archive contains 1,106 tornado records, 3,064 injuries, and 221 fatalities for Michigan. These totals describe documented events rather than the probability at a particular address.

The longest path recorded in the state dataset is 80.5 miles on April 11, 1965. Path length can cross county or state lines and does not describe width or intensity.

When Michigan tornadoes happen

June leads the historical monthly count with 250 records. January has the smallest count, at 1. Seasonal peaks reflect recurring weather patterns, but every year evolves differently.

Use the monthly chart as climatology, not a calendar guarantee. Tornadoes can occur whenever sufficient instability, moisture, lift, and wind shear overlap.

How residents should use the history

Know the safest room in every building you use, enable more than one warning source, and check the local forecast when severe weather is possible. During a warning, the current polygon matters more than the historical average.

Mobile homes and vehicles are especially vulnerable. Plans for those locations should identify sturdy shelter that can be reached before a warning.

Frequently asked questions

What month has the most tornadoes in Michigan?

June has the largest count in the SPC archive used here, with 250 recorded tornadoes. Individual years can differ sharply.

How many tornadoes have been recorded in Michigan?

The archive contains 1,106 records from 1951 through 2024. The total reflects reporting and database practices as well as weather.

What is the strongest tornado recorded in Michigan?

The highest rating in this archive is F/EF5. Fujita and Enhanced Fujita ratings are damage-based and are not directly interchangeable wind measurements.

Data source and limits

Counts on this page come from the NOAA Storm Prediction Center historical tornado archive for 1951–2024. Older records are less complete, weaker tornado reporting increased with population and technology, and ratings changed from the Fujita scale to the Enhanced Fujita scale in 2007. A record is not a forecast.
