Can you find the updraft in a supercell cross-section?

Trace three arrows through an illustrated storm and identify which one carries air upward.

Tornado Hub · November 20, 2026

Choose A, B or C

  1. An arrow runs near the ground toward the storm.
  2. An arrow rises from the base through the tilted cloud.
  3. An arrow descends from the cloud toward the ground.
Reveal the annotated answer

B is the updraft. A is near-surface inflow into the storm, and C is a downdraft. The colored overlay marks B orange, A pale blue and C cyan.

Follow the airflow through time

Open the cross-section at minute 30. Step back to lift or forward to decay. Rising air marks the updraft throughout the illustration, even as the shape of the cloud changes.
