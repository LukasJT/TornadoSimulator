Tornado Safety at Hospitals

Hospitals face extraordinary tornado safety challenges: bed-bound patients, critical medical equipment, and multi-story structures. Modern hospitals include specialized shelter areas and coordinated emergency response plans that have saved thousands of lives during tornado events.
