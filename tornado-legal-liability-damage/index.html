Contractor scams

  • Storm chasers (contractors, not weather chasers).
  • Fly-by-night operations.
  • Unlicensed contractors.
  • Cash-only demanding.
  • Assignment of Benefits abuse.
  • Overcharging.
  • Substandard work.
  • Disappearing before completion.
  • False claims.
  • Elderly targeting.

Contractor law protection

  • State licensing verification.
  • Written contracts required.
  • Never pay more than 10% upfront.
  • Never pay cash.
  • Progress payments only after work verified.
  • Retainage for final work.
  • Right to cancel period (3 days typical).
  • BBB and state consumer protection.
  • Attorney if disputes.

Assignment of Benefits (AOB)

  • Contractor takes over insurance payment.
  • You sign over rights to insurance proceeds.
  • Sometimes leads to lawsuits.
  • Consumer protection issues.
  • Some states restrict.
  • Read what you sign.
  • Consult attorney before signing.
  • Ethics of contractor.

FEMA assistance

  • Presidential disaster declaration required.
  • Individual Assistance for households.
  • Public Assistance for governments.
  • Grants and low-interest loans.
  • SBA disaster loans.
  • Application at disasterassistance.gov.
  • Documentation extensive.
  • Time limits.
  • Appeal process.
  • Coordinate with insurance.

  2. Days 4-14: Insurance claim filing.
  3. Days 14-30: Initial contractor engagement.
  4. Days 30-90: Insurance settlement.
  5. Months 3-6: Rebuild permits.
  6. Months 6-24: Rebuild work.
  7. Year 2+: Legal disputes if any.
