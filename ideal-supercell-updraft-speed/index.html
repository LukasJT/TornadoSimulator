Why doubling CAPE falls short of doubling speed

Equating buoyant energy per kilogram with kinetic energy per kilogram gives w = √(2 × CAPE). Doubling CAPE therefore multiplies ideal speed by √2, about 1.414. Going from 500 to 2500 J/kg multiplies speed by √5, about 2.24. Entrainment and water loading affect real updrafts, so treat these values as ideal parcel estimates.
