How do you calculate the density of a warm inflow parcel?

Tornado Hub · Lesson · Atmospheric science · algebra · November 21, 2026

Calculate dry-air density by dividing pressure in pascals by the dry-air gas constant times absolute temperature.

Start with Hydrostatic pressure practice problems for a storm sounding.

Advertisement

Worked example

The ideal gas law gives ρ = p/(RᵈT), where Rᵈ = 287 J/(kg K). At 900 hPa and 300 K, convert pressure to 90,000 Pa and divide by 86,100 J/kg. The density is about 1.05 kg/m³. For moist air use virtual temperature in place of actual temperature.

Practice questions

  1. At 1,000 hPa and 300 K, find dry density.
  2. At the same pressure, what happens if temperature increases by 10% in kelvins?

Worked answers

Answer 1

100,000/86,100 ≈ 1.16 kg/m³.

Answer 2

Density is divided by 1.10 and falls by about 9.1%.
