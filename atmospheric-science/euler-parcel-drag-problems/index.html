Euler time stepping for a rising parcel with drag: worksheet

Tornado Hub · Practice set · Applied math · introductory numerics · November 21, 2026

Explicit Euler integration updates parcel speed by adding the current acceleration times the time step. Evaluate acceleration at the start of the step.

Start with Does a midpoint time step improve a parcel trajectory?.

Advertisement

Worked example

Take upward positive and a(w) = 0.4−0.02w in SI units. At w = 10 m/s, a = 0.20 m/s². Over five seconds Euler adds 1 m/s and gives 11 m/s. The midpoint or exact solution can differ because the acceleration changes as speed evolves.

Practice questions

  1. Evaluate a at 10 m/s.
  2. Compute the new speed after the first five seconds.
  3. Take a second five-second step.
  4. Find the equilibrium speed for the prescribed acceleration law.
  5. A student writes wnew = w+a with a five-second interval. Correct it.
  6. What happens to the acceleration above equilibrium speed?

Worked answers

Answer 1

0.4−0.02×10 = 0.20 m/s².

Answer 2

10+0.20×5 = 11 m/s.

Answer 3

At 11 m/s the acceleration is 0.18 m/s². The next speed is 11.9 m/s.

Answer 4

Solve 0.4−0.02w = 0. The result is 20 m/s.

Answer 5

Multiply acceleration by Δt so the increment has units of velocity.

Answer 6

It becomes negative, slowing an upward-moving parcel in this example.
