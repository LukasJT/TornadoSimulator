How do you build a two-factor supercell simulation experiment?

Tornado Hub · Lab · Storm modeling · algebra and sensitivity · November 21, 2026

A two-factor experiment changes two inputs across matched levels. Compare the effect of one factor at both levels of the other to measure interaction in the index response.

Start with How do you reproduce a simulator result from a sounding with missing inputs?.

Set up the experiment

Hold the LCL and helicity factors at one. Compare CAPE factors 1 and 2 and shear factors 0.75 and 1.25, corresponding to CAPE of 1,500 and 3,000 J/kg and shear of 15 and 25 m/s. Predict whether the CAPE increase will yield the same absolute change at both shear levels. Multiply each pair. The CAPE effect is 0.75 at low shear and 1.25 at high shear. The difference is 0.50 index units. This interaction follows from the formula and the scale on which it is measured.

Practice questions

  1. Find the low-CAPE effect of raising shear.
  2. Find the high-CAPE effect of raising shear.
  3. Repeat when the LCL factor is 0.5 in all four runs.

Worked answers

Answer 1

1.25−0.75 = 0.50.

Answer 2

2.50−1.50 = 1.00.

Answer 3

All products halve and the absolute interaction becomes 0.25.

  1. 1.25−0.75 = 0.50.
  2. 2.50−1.50 = 1.00.
  3. All products halve and the absolute interaction becomes 0.25.
