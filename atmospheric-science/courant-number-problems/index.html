Worked example

At speed 25 m/s and spacing 500 m, the largest step under that limit is 500/25 = 20 s. Compute using the speed magnitude. The neighboring cell comes from upstream, which changes with the sign of U. Other numerical schemes or model processes can impose different limits.

Practice questions

  1. Find the Courant number at 10 s.
  2. Find it at 20 s.
  3. With the same speed and 250 m spacing, find the largest step.
  4. With 500 m spacing and a 30 s step, find the largest speed.
  5. A student treats negative U as permission for any step. Correct that assumption.
  6. Does passing this test guarantee accuracy?

Worked answers

Answer 1

25×10/500 = 0.5.

Answer 2

25×20/500 = 1.

Answer 3

250/25 = 10 s.

Answer 4

500/30 ≈ 16.67 m/s.

Answer 5

Use its magnitude in the Courant number and change the upstream direction.
