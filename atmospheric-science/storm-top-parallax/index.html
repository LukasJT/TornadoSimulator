How much does satellite viewing angle displace a tall storm top?

Tornado Hub · Lesson · Environmental science · measurement · November 21, 2026

A satellite viewing a cloud off nadir can place the cloud top away from its actual ground location. In a local flat approximation, the offset magnitude is height times the tangent of the viewing zenith angle.

Worked example

With cloud top height 12 km and viewing angle 45° from local vertical, h tan θ gives 12 km. At nadir the angle is zero and the approximate horizontal shift vanishes. Detailed navigation accounts for Earth geometry and the viewing direction.

Practice questions

  1. Find the offset at 30° for the same cloud.
  2. If the angle stays fixed and the estimated cloud height doubles, what happens?

Worked answers

Answer 1

12 tan 30° ≈ 6.93 km.

Answer 2

The modeled offset doubles.
