How do you estimate circulation around a rotating updraft?

Tornado Hub · Lesson · Atmospheric science · vector calculus · November 21, 2026

Circulation is the line integral of velocity along a closed loop. For a circle of radius r with constant counterclockwise tangential speed V, circulation is 2πrV.

Worked example

Take the positive loop direction counterclockwise viewed from above. For r = 500 m and V = 10 m/s, Γ ≈ 31,416 m²/s. Stokes’ theorem gives the area-average vertical vorticity as Γ/(πr²), here 0.040 s⁻¹. The boundary measurement determines the mean without resolving the local vorticity everywhere inside.

Practice questions

  1. Double the radius at fixed 10 m/s. Find Γ and mean ζ.
  2. How does reversing the flow affect the answers at fixed radius?

Worked answers

Answer 1

Γ doubles to about 62,832 m²/s. The mean falls to 0.020 s⁻¹ because area quadruples.

Answer 2

It changes the signs of both circulation and mean vorticity and leaves their magnitudes unchanged.
