How do you find layer thickness with the hypsometric equation?

Tornado Hub · Lesson · Atmospheric science · algebra · November 21, 2026

The hypsometric equation estimates the vertical distance between two pressure surfaces from their pressure ratio and the layer’s mean virtual temperature.

Start with Potential temperature practice problems with dry parcels.

Worked example

Substitute ρ = p/(RᵈTᵥ) into hydrostatic balance and integrate with respect to log pressure. Thickness is (Rᵈ/g) mean(Tᵥ) ln(pbottom/ptop), using a log-pressure mean. For 1,000 to 900 hPa and 280 K, with g = 9.81 m/s², the result is about 863 m.

Practice questions

  1. What happens if the layer cools from 280 to 270 K at the same pressure boundaries?
  2. Why should both pressure values use the same unit?

Worked answers

Answer 1

Thickness scales with temperature, giving about 832 m.

Answer 2

The ratio must be dimensionless. Using Pa for both gives the same ratio as hPa for both.
