Can two environments have the same STP and different ideal updraft speeds?

Tornado Hub · Lab · Storm modeling · algebra and sensitivity · November 21, 2026

Two environments can share a fixed-layer STP while having different CAPE. The ideal parcel speed depends only on CAPE in TornadoSim’s simplified energy calculation.

Start with How do you build a two-factor supercell simulation experiment?.

Set up the experiment

Keep shear at 20 m/s and LCL at 1,000 m AGL. Before calculating, predict which combination has the larger theoretical speed and whether the index changes. The products of normalized CAPE and helicity are 1×1 and 2×0.5, both one. The theoretical speeds are √3000 and √6000 m/s. The square-root relation describes lossless parcel acceleration, so actual updrafts can differ.

Practice questions

  1. By what ratio did ideal speed change?
  2. At 750 J/kg, what SRH retains an index of one under the same assumptions?
  3. What happens if LCL rises to 1,500 m in both runs?

Worked answers

Answer 1

√2 ≈ 1.414.

Answer 2

CAPE factor is 0.5, so the helicity factor must be two and SRH must be 300 m²/s².

Answer 3

Both indices halve and the ideal speeds stay the same.
