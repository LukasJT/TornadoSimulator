How is supercell rotation estimated from an inbound–outbound velocity pair?

Tornado Hub · Lesson · Physical science · waves and logarithms · November 21, 2026

Half the difference between paired outbound and inbound Doppler velocities provides a simple radial rotation proxy in a velocity couplet.

Start with Differential reflectivity practice: what does positive ZDR mean?.

Advertisement

Worked example

Treat outward motion as positive. For −20 and +30 m/s, half the difference is 25 m/s. The midpoint of the two values is +5 m/s. The proxy depends on radar viewing angle and resolution. Beam spreading can hide true extrema, and other flow gradients can resemble rotation.

Practice questions

  1. Find the proxy for −15 and +15 m/s.
  2. Add +8 m/s to both original values. What changes?

Worked answers

Answer 1

(15−(−15))/2 = 15 m/s.

Answer 2

The midpoint rises to 13 m/s. The half difference remains 25 m/s.
