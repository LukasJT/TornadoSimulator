How do LCL height limits change the fixed-layer STP in the simulator?

Tornado Hub · Lab · Storm modeling · algebra and sensitivity · November 21, 2026

In TornadoSim’s fixed-layer significant tornado parameter, the cloud-base factor decreases linearly between 1,000 and 2,000 m AGL. It is clamped to one below that range and zero above it.

Set up the experiment

Hold CAPE at 1,500 J/kg, helicity at 150 m²/s² and shear at 45 kt. Before graphing, predict the shape when cloud base varies. Compute the cloud-base factor by clamping (2000−LCL)/1000 to the range 0–1. The cloud-base factors are one, one-half and zero for the three test heights. At 45 kt the other factors multiply to about 1.157. The index describes an environment and is dimensionless.

Practice questions

  1. What factor do you get at 1,250 m?
  2. Compare results at 500 and 800 m.
  3. What if the computed LCL is 2,100 m and CAPE doubles?

Worked answers

Answer 1

The unclamped value is 0.75, which is within bounds.

Answer 2

Both give one because the calculation is capped.

Answer 3

The factor stays zero and the product stays zero.
