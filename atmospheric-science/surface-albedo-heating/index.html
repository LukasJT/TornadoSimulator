How does reflectivity of the ground affect absorbed sunlight before convection?

Tornado Hub · Lesson · Environmental science · budgets · November 21, 2026

A surface with lower albedo absorbs more incoming sunlight when the incident shortwave flux is the same.

Start with How do you estimate moisture flux through a storm inflow boundary?.

Worked example

Use absorbed shortwave flux S(1−α), with incoming flux S and albedo α between zero and one. At 600 W/m², α = 0.20 gives 480 W/m², while α = 0.60 gives 240 W/m². This compares shortwave absorption. Soil heating, net longwave radiation and turbulent fluxes govern how much energy warms the near-surface air.

Practice questions

  1. Find the absorbed flux at albedo 0.35.
  2. At what albedo does this supplied daylight flux yield 300 W/m² of absorption?

Worked answers

Answer 1

600×0.65 = 390 W/m².

Answer 2

1−α = 300/600 = 0.5, so α = 0.5.

  1. 600×0.65 = 390 W/m².
  2. 1−α = 300/600 = 0.5, so α = 0.5.
