How does a pressure perturbation accelerate air inside a supercell?

Tornado Hub · Lesson · Atmospheric science · vector calculus · November 21, 2026

A perturbation pressure gradient accelerates air toward lower perturbation pressure. Divide the negative gradient by density to obtain acceleration.

Start with Why can a splitting supercell move away from the mean wind?.

Advertisement

Worked example

If pressure perturbation decreases eastward by 120 Pa over 2,000 m, its east derivative is −0.060 Pa/m. Using density 1.2 kg/m³, the east acceleration is −(−0.060)/1.2 = +0.050 m/s². This is a local contribution. Total storm acceleration may include other terms.

Practice questions

  1. Reverse the pressure gradient and keep density fixed.
  2. Keep the original gradient and lower density to 1.0 kg/m³.

Worked answers

Answer 1

The acceleration becomes −0.050 m/s², toward the west.

Answer 2

Acceleration increases to +0.060 m/s².

  1. The acceleration becomes −0.050 m/s², toward the west.
  2. Acceleration increases to +0.060 m/s².
