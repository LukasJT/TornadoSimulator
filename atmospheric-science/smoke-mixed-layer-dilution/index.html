How does turbulent mixing dilute smoke in a deepening mixed layer?

Tornado Hub · Lesson · Environmental science · budgets · November 21, 2026

Mixing a fixed smoke mass into a deeper layer lowers its mean concentration when the added air is clean.

Start with How do you calculate runoff volume after supercell rainfall?.

Advertisement

Worked example

For a unit horizontal area, assume the smoke mass stays fixed and the original 300 m slab is uniform at 60 μg/m³. Its column mass is 18,000 μg/m². Mix uniformly to 900 m with clean air to get 20 μg/m³. This idealization omits emissions, removal and horizontal transport.

Practice questions

  1. Find the mean concentration at 600 m with otherwise identical assumptions.
  2. What if the added 600 m contains 10 μg/m³ initially?

Worked answers

Answer 1

18,000/600 = 30 μg/m³.

Answer 2

The total column mass is 24,000 μg/m². The final concentration is about 26.7 μg/m³.

  1. 18,000/600 = 30 μg/m³.
  2. The total column mass is 24,000 μg/m². The final concentration is about 26.7 μg/m³.
