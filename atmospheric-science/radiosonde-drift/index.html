How far downwind can a radiosonde drift while sampling a storm environment?

Tornado Hub · Lesson · Environmental science · measurement · November 21, 2026

A radiosonde drifts with the horizontal wind during ascent. For a uniform wind, multiply that velocity by the ascent time.

Start with How do you decode sea-level pressure from a station plot?.

Worked example

At a constant climb rate of 5 m/s, reaching 3,000 m above launch takes 600 s. In a uniform eastward wind of 12 m/s, the balloon drifts 7,200 m east. For height-varying winds, calculate time in each layer and add vector displacements.

Practice questions

  1. Repeat with climb rate 6 m/s.
  2. What if the wind reverses direction at the halfway height with unchanged speed?

Worked answers

Answer 1

3,000/6 = 500 s, giving 6 km east.

Answer 2

Equal times and opposite velocities cancel, giving no net horizontal displacement in the idealization.
