How do you track an updraft centroid in model output?

Tornado Hub · Lab · Storm modeling · upper undergraduate · November 21, 2026

Find an updraft centroid by averaging the positions of cells assigned to the updraft. A weighted centroid shifts toward cells with larger assigned weights.

Start with How do you check water-mass conservation in a cloud model?.

Advertisement

Set up the experiment

Keep the same threshold and two included cells at x = 0 and 2 km. Give them weights 10 and 30. Predict which side the weighted centre will favor. The centroid is (0×10+2×30)/(10+30) = 1.5 km, while the unweighted value is 1 km. In two dimensions apply the same normalization to x and y.

Practice questions

  1. Double both weights.
  2. Exclude cell A by raising the threshold.
  3. What should you report if no cell passes the threshold?

Worked answers

Answer 1

The centroid stays at 1.5 km because numerator and denominator both double.

Answer 2

Cell B alone gives 2 km.

Answer 3

The centroid is unavailable. The denominator is zero and there is no diagnosed updraft in the selected mask.
