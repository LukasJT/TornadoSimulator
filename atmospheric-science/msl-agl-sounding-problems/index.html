How do you convert sounding heights from MSL to AGL?

Tornado Hub · Practice set · Environmental science · measurement · November 21, 2026

To convert an altitude referenced to mean sea level into height above the sounding station, subtract station elevation.

Start with How far downwind can a radiosonde drift while sampling a storm environment?.

Advertisement

Worked example

Use zAGL = zMSL − zstation with both elevations in the same units and vertical datum. A sample at 1,710 m MSL from a site at 460 m MSL lies 1,250 m above the launch station. For a drifting balloon, height above local terrain along the trajectory can differ from height above its starting site.

Practice questions

  1. Find the sample height above the 460 m station.
  2. Convert the launch point’s own altitude to AGL.
  3. What altitude MSL is 3 km AGL at this site?
  4. At a coastal site 20 m below mean sea level, find AGL at 180 m MSL.
  5. A student adds the 460 m elevation to 1,710 m. Correct the reference shift.
  6. Why can a balloon have a different height above nearby terrain later in flight?

Worked answers

Answer 1

1710−460 = 1250 m.

Answer 2

460−460 = 0 m.

Answer 3

3000+460 = 3460 m MSL.

Answer 4

180−(−20) = 200 m.

Answer 5

Subtract the ground offset from an MSL altitude to move the zero to ground.

Answer 6

Its horizontal position can change over terrain at a different elevation.
