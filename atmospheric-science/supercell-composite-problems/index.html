How do you calculate the supercell composite parameter with effective-layer inputs?

Tornado Hub · Practice set · Atmospheric science · advanced diagnostics · November 21, 2026

The supercell composite parameter multiplies normalized most-unstable CAPE, effective storm-relative helicity and an effective bulk shear factor. Use the exact variant and layer definitions from your reference implementation.

Start with Why do effective-layer and fixed-layer tornado parameters differ?.

Worked example

For the referenced MetPy convention, the normalized CAPE factor is MUCAPE/1000 and the helicity factor is effective SRH/50. Shear below 10 m/s sets its factor to zero. From 10 through 20 m/s use shear divided by 20, with a cap of one. At 2,000 J/kg, 100 m²/s² and 25 m/s the factors are two, two and one. SCP = 4.
