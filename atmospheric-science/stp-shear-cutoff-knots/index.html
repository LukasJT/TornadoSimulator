What happens at the knot-to-metre-per-second STP shear cutoff?

Tornado Hub · Lab · Storm modeling · algebra and sensitivity · November 21, 2026

Use metres per second when comparing shear to the simulator’s 12.5 m/s lower threshold. A one-knot step can cross the cutoff.

Start with Can two environments have the same STP and different ideal updraft speeds?.

Set up the experiment

Keep CAPE at 1,500 J/kg, cloud base at 1,000 m AGL and helicity at 150 m²/s². Predict which shear value will make the index zero. Multiplying by 0.514444 gives 12.3467 m/s for 24 kt and 12.8611 m/s for 25 kt. The site assigns zero below 12.5 m/s and otherwise uses shear divided by 20 up to a cap of 1.5. Treat the jump as a feature of the index formula.

Practice questions

  1. What factor applies at exactly 12.5 m/s?
  2. Convert 20 m/s to knots.
  3. What is the shear factor at 65 kt?

Worked answers

Answer 1

The below-threshold condition is strict. The factor is 12.5/20 = 0.625.

Answer 2

20/0.514444 ≈ 38.88 kt.

Answer 3

The speed is about 33.44 m/s and the factor is capped at 1.5.
