Why do effective-layer and fixed-layer tornado parameters differ?

Tornado Hub · Lesson · Atmospheric science · advanced diagnostics · November 21, 2026

Fixed-layer and effective-layer tornado parameters use different parcel and wind-layer definitions. Their values can diverge even when both are calculated correctly for the same sounding.

Start with How do you compute the bulk Richardson number for convection?.

Worked example

The TornadoSim fixed-layer version uses surface-based CAPE, surface-parcel cloud base, 0–1 km SRH and 0–6 km bulk shear. The commonly cited effective-layer variant uses mixed-layer parcel thermodynamics, including a CIN term, and effective inflow and shear diagnostics. A summary listing only surface CAPE and 0–1 km SRH lacks enough information to recover the effective-layer version. Identify both formulas when comparing values.

Worked answers

Answer 1

The surface and mixed-layer parcels can have different moisture, buoyancy and inhibition. Effective inflow can select different layer bounds.

Obtain the thermodynamic and wind profiles, storm motion and exact parcel, layer and factor definitions.
