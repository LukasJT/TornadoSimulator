Why does baroclinicity generate vorticity along storm outflow?

Tornado Hub · Lesson · Atmospheric science · dynamics · November 21, 2026

Baroclinic vorticity production occurs when pressure and density gradients point in different directions. The cross product gives the direction of the resulting local tendency.

Worked example

For density gradient eastward and pressure gradient northward, use (∇ρ×∇p)/ρ². In a right-handed east–north–up system, east crossed with north points upward. Parallel or antiparallel gradients give zero. Boundaries between cold outflow and nearby warmer air can support misaligned gradients.

Practice questions

  1. Keep the density gradient eastward and turn the pressure gradient southward.
  2. When are fixed-magnitude gradients most effective at producing a cross product?

Worked answers

Answer 1

The cross product points down.

Answer 2

When they are perpendicular, because the sine of their angle is one.

  1. The cross product points down.
  2. When they are perpendicular, because the sine of their angle is one.
