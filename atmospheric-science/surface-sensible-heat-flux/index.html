How do you calculate sensible heat flux beneath a developing storm?

Tornado Hub · Lesson · Environmental science · budgets · November 21, 2026

The bulk sensible heat equation estimates the upward transfer from the surface to the air. It scales with wind speed and the surface-to-air temperature difference.

Start with How much evaporation does a given latent heat flux represent?.

Advertisement

Worked example

Use H = ρcpCₕU(Ts−Ta), where ρ is air density, cp is specific heat, Cₕ is a dimensionless transfer coefficient and U is wind speed at the specified reference height. With 1.2 kg/m³, 1000 J/(kg K), 0.001, 5 m/s and 4 K, H = +24 W/m². This exercise prescribes the coefficient, which can vary with roughness and stability.

Practice questions

  1. Double wind speed while holding the coefficient fixed.
  2. What does a negative result indicate here?

Worked answers

Answer 1

The estimate doubles to 48 W/m².

Answer 2

The modeled sensible transfer is toward the surface.
