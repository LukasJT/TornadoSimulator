How does an upwind scheme move a moisture pulse?

Tornado Hub · Lesson · Applied math · introductory numerics · November 21, 2026

An explicit upwind step replaces part of a cell’s old value with the upstream cell’s value. With positive flow, the upstream cell lies to its left.

Start with Central-difference practice using a storm pressure field.

Worked example

Set qnew = qhere − C(qhere−qupwind), where C = UΔt/Δx for positive U. If C = 0.25, qhere = 8 and qupwind = 12 g/kg, the new value is 8−0.25(8−12) = 9 g/kg. The update is a weighted average for 0≤C≤1 and can blur sharp gradients.

Practice questions

  1. What if C becomes 0.5?
  2. If the upstream cell is 4 g/kg at C = 0.25, what happens?

Worked answers

Answer 1

The update gives the mean of the two original values, 10 g/kg.

Answer 2

The local value falls to 8−0.25(8−4) = 7 g/kg.

  1. The update gives the mean of the two original values, 10 g/kg.
  2. The local value falls to 8−0.25(8−4) = 7 g/kg.
