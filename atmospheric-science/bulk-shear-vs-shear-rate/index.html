What is the difference between bulk shear and shear rate?

Tornado Hub · Lesson · Physical science · vectors · November 21, 2026

Bulk shear describes the vector change across a layer in m/s. Mean vertical shear rate divides that change by the layer depth and has units of s⁻¹.

Worked example

If the eastward wind component increases by 15 m/s over 1 km, the mean east-component shear rate is 15/1,000 = 0.015 s⁻¹. Spread the same change over 3 km and the rate is 15/3,000 = 0.005 s⁻¹. A mean rate over a layer can differ from a local derivative when the wind profile curves.

Practice questions

  1. The northward component drops by 4 m/s over 500 m. Find its mean shear rate.
  2. Which quantity stays unchanged when the same vector difference is distributed over twice the depth?

Worked answers

Answer 1

−4/500 = −0.008 s⁻¹.

Answer 2

Bulk shear stays fixed. The mean shear rate is halved.

  1. −4/500 = −0.008 s⁻¹.
  2. Bulk shear stays fixed. The mean shear rate is halved.
