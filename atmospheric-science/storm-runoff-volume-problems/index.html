How do you calculate runoff volume after supercell rainfall?

Tornado Hub · Practice set · Environmental science · budgets · November 21, 2026

Multiply rainfall depth by watershed area to find rain volume. Then multiply by an assumed runoff fraction to estimate runoff volume.

Start with How does turbulent mixing dilute smoke in a deepening mixed layer?.

Worked example

At 25 mm over 4 km², convert to 0.025 m and 4,000,000 m². Uniform rainfall supplies 100,000 m³. A runoff fraction of 0.40 gives 40,000 m³. This is an event volume estimate. A peak discharge requires timing, routing and storage information.

Practice questions

  1. Convert the rain depth and basin area into SI units.
  2. Find the total rain volume and runoff volume.
  3. Repeat at 15 mm and runoff fraction 0.25 over the same area.
  4. Find the equivalent runoff depth for 40,000 m³ spread across the original basin.
  5. A student converts 4 km² to 4,000 m². What went wrong?
  6. Can the same runoff volume produce different peak flows?

Worked answers

Answer 1

Use 0.025 m and 4×10⁶ m².

Answer 2

Rain volume is 100,000 m³. Runoff volume is 40,000 m³.

Answer 3

0.015×4×10⁶×0.25 = 15,000 m³.

Answer 4

40,000/(4×10⁶) = 0.010 m, or 10 mm.

Answer 5

Both horizontal dimensions convert by 1,000, so the area factor is 1,000,000.

Answer 6

Yes. Faster delivery can produce a higher peak even at equal total volume.
