How do you integrate updraft helicity on a model grid?

Tornado Hub · Lab · Atmospheric science · advanced diagnostics · November 21, 2026

Updraft helicity integrates the product of vertical velocity and vertical vorticity over a specified height layer in model output.

Start with How do you compute the bulk Richardson number for convection?.

Advertisement

Set up the experiment

Keep w = 10 m/s and ζ = 0.01 s⁻¹ constant from 2 to 5 km AGL. Predict the sign from the two factors and convert the depth to 3,000 m. The assigned integral is 10×0.01×3,000 = +300 m²/s². Real diagnostics can use model-specific staggering, boundaries and sign filters. Document these before comparing values.

Practice questions

  1. Repeat with w = 5 m/s and the same vorticity.
  2. Use w = 10 m/s and ζ = −0.01 s⁻¹ in the signed exercise.
  3. Why specify the integration layer?

Worked answers

Answer 1

5×0.01×3,000 = +150 m²/s².

Answer 2

The result is −300 m²/s² before any implementation-specific sign filtering.

Answer 3

The product can vary strongly with height. Values from different layers need not describe the same modeled feature.
