When can a storm-scale model neglect Earth’s rotation?

Tornado Hub · Lesson · Atmospheric science · dynamics · November 21, 2026

The Rossby number compares inertial acceleration with Coriolis acceleration. Rotation is often a smaller direct contribution when the number is much greater than one.

Start with How do you calculate horizontal temperature advection?.

Worked example

Use Ro = U/(|f|L). With U = 20 m/s, |f| = 10⁻⁴ s⁻¹ and L = 10 km, Ro = 20. At 1,000 km it is 0.20. Choose scales that represent the motion of interest. Storm-scale flow can coexist with an environment shaped by rotation on larger scales.

Practice questions

  1. At what length is Ro = 1 in the given setting?
  2. What happens if wind speed halves at fixed L and f?

Worked answers

Answer 1

L = 20/0.0001 = 200 km.

Answer 2

Ro halves, making rotation comparatively more important.
