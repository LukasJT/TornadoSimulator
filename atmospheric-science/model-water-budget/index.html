How do you check water-mass conservation in a cloud model?

Tornado Hub · Lab · Storm modeling · upper undergraduate · November 21, 2026

Check water conservation by comparing simulated final mass with initial mass plus gains minus losses. State which forms of water and boundary fluxes are included.

Start with How does changing wind shear alter idealized storm organization at fixed buoyancy?.

Set up the experiment

Hold the volume and accounting window fixed. Start at 1,000 kg, add 80 kg and remove 30 kg. Predict the final mass if the budget closes. It should be 1,050 kg. An observed model total of 1,048 kg leaves −2 kg, or −0.19% of expected mass. Count exchanges consistently across the boundary and avoid double-counting transfers between phases.

Practice questions

  1. Find the residual if the model instead ends at 1,052 kg.
  2. An additional 2 kg precipitation loss was omitted. Recalculate the expected final mass.
  3. How can a falling hydrometeor transfer affect the total?

Worked answers

Answer 1

It becomes +2 kg, about +0.19%.

Answer 2

1,048 kg. Using the original model final mass, the revised residual is zero.

Answer 3

A phase change within the domain leaves total water unchanged. Water leaving through the boundary is a loss.
