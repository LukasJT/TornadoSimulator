What does an infrared brightness temperature tell you about a storm top?

Tornado Hub · Lesson · Environmental science · measurement · November 21, 2026

For an opaque infrared cloud, matching brightness temperature to an environmental temperature profile can give an approximate emission height.

Start with How do you estimate visible-light transmission through a cloud?.

Advertisement

Worked example

Assume 250 K at 8 km and 230 K at 10 km, with temperature linear in between. A brightness temperature of 240 K is halfway between the two readings, implying an emission height of about 9 km. Thin clouds, temperature inversions and radiative transfer effects can defeat the simple mapping.

Practice questions

  1. Estimate the level for 245 K.
  2. Why does an inversion complicate the lookup?

Worked answers

Answer 1

It is one-quarter of the way up the interval, or 8.5 km.

Answer 2

A temperature can occur at more than one height. Match it to other observations before choosing an emission level.
