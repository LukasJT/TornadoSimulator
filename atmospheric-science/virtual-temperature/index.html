How much does water vapor change a parcel’s virtual temperature?

Tornado Hub · Lesson · Atmospheric science · algebra · November 21, 2026

Virtual temperature is the dry-air temperature that gives the same density as moist air at the same pressure.

Start with Mixing ratio vs. specific humidity: conversion exercises.

Worked example

For ordinary water-vapor fractions and no condensate, use Tᵥ ≈ T(1+0.61q), where q is kilograms of vapor per kilogram of moist air. At 300 K and q = 0.010 kg/kg the result is 301.83 K. The higher virtual temperature gives lower density at fixed pressure.

Practice questions

  1. Find Tᵥ at 300 K and 20 g/kg.
  2. How does the 10 g/kg parcel’s density compare with dry air at equal pressure and actual temperature?

Worked answers

Answer 1

Convert to 0.020 kg/kg. Then Tᵥ ≈ 303.66 K.

Answer 2

The ratio is 300/301.83 ≈ 0.994. The moist parcel is about 0.6% less dense.
