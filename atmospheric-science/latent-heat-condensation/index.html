How much latent heat is released as water condenses inside an updraft?

Tornado Hub · Lesson · Environmental science · algebra · November 21, 2026

Condensation releases approximately the condensate mass times the latent heat of vaporization. Work per kilogram of air when the water amount is specified as a mass ratio.

Worked example

For 2 g of condensed vapor per kilogram of air, use 0.002 kg/kg. Multiply by the assumed latent heat Lᵥ of 2.5×10⁶ J/kg of water to obtain 5,000 J/kg of air. The latent heat varies with temperature. A temperature prediction would also require an energy budget for expansion and mixing.

Practice questions

  1. How much energy is released when 0.4 g/kg condenses?
  2. How much energy is released in an air mass of 200 kg under the original assumption?

Worked answers

Answer 1

0.0004×2.5×10⁶ = 1,000 J/kg of air.

Answer 2

200×5,000 = 1,000,000 J.
