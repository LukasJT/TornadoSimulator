What does a surface Bowen ratio tell you about storm inflow?

Tornado Hub · Practice set · Environmental science · budgets · November 21, 2026

The Bowen ratio is sensible heat flux divided by latent heat flux. Together with their sum it determines how that energy is partitioned.

Start with How does reflectivity of the ground affect absorbed sunlight before convection?.

Advertisement

Worked example

Define β = H/LE and available turbulent flux F = H + LE after any other budget terms have been accounted for. Then LE = F/(1+β) and H = βLE. With F = 300 W/m² and β = 0.5, LE = 200 W/m² and H = 100 W/m².

Practice questions

  1. Find LE for the first example.
  2. Find H in the same example.
  3. Repeat at the same sum with β = 2.
  4. If H = 60 and LE = 240 W/m², find the ratio and sensible share.
  5. A student multiplies the total by 0.5 to find sensible heat for β = 0.5. Correct the denominator.
  6. Can you calculate an ordinary finite ratio if LE is zero?

Worked answers

Answer 1

300/(1+0.5) = 200 W/m².

Answer 2

300−200 = 100 W/m².

Answer 3

LE = 100 W/m² and H = 200 W/m².

Answer 4

β = 0.25, and H/(H+LE) = 0.20.

Answer 5

The ratio is to latent flux. The sensible share of the total is β/(1+β) = 1/3.

Answer 6

No. LE is the denominator, so the ratio is undefined at zero latent flux.
