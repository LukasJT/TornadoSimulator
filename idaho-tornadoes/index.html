Idaho Tornadoes

Idaho averages 5 tornadoes per year - one of the lowest counts in the country. Mountain terrain and semi-arid climate suppress most tornado formation. Southern Idaho occasionally experiences weak tornadoes.

Idaho Tornado Statistics

  • Average tornadoes per year: ~5
  • Peak season: Summer
  • Most tornadoes: EF0-EF1

Why So Rare?

Mountain Terrain

Rocky Mountains dominate. Terrain suppresses tornado formation.

Semi-Arid Climate

Dry air limits convection potential.

Elevation

Higher elevations limit tornado dynamics.

Regional Idaho Risk

Southern Idaho

Boise area. Snake River Plain sees occasional events.

Central Idaho

Rugged mountain terrain. Very rare tornadoes.

Northern Idaho

Mountains and forests. Rare tornadoes.

Living in Idaho

  • Tornado risk very low
  • Basic weather awareness sufficient
  • Focus on other severe weather
  • NOAA weather radio for general severe weather
