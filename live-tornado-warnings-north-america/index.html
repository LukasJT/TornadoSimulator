Radar and warning coverage

The radar layer is the 1-km rain-rate composite from Environment and Climate Change Canada’s MSC GeoMet service. Scan timestamps come from the feed. Blank areas may have no detected precipitation or limited radar coverage. U.S. and Canadian warning areas come from their respective weather agencies.

Reading SPC outlooks

Day 1 and Day 2 tornado outlooks show the probability of a tornado within 25 miles of a point for the stated valid period. They cover the contiguous United States. Read the official SPC outlook for the full explanation.
