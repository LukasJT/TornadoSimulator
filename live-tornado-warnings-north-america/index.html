What the colors mean

Live warnings mark official tornado-warning areas. Risk outlooks show the probability of a tornado within 25 miles of a point during the valid period. A hatched SPC area indicates a 10% or greater chance of a significant tornado within 25 miles.

Coverage and timing

United States: National Weather Service warnings and SPC outlooks. Canada: Environment and Climate Change Canada warnings. Mexico remains visible for geographic context, but no compatible official tornado polygon feed is represented.
