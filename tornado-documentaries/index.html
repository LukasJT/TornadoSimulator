Watching list

Best tornado documentaries

A curated list of the best tornado documentaries ever made — where to watch, what they cover, and which are worth your time.

Classic public-television docs

NOVA: Hunt for the Supertwister (2004)
The VORTEX-1 field experiment. PBS.
NOVA: Deadliest Tornadoes (2012)
2011 Super Outbreak analysis.
NOVA: Killer Storm (2017)
Post-2011 focus on Joplin.
BBC Horizon: The Perfect Storm
British take on Plains chasing.

Discovery Channel era

Storm Chasers (2007-2011)
Reed Timmer, Sean Casey, TWISTEX. 5 seasons.
Tornado Chasers (2012-2014)
Reed Timmer's follow-up. Web-first.
Tornado Alley (2011)
Sean Casey IMAX film. TIV vs Vortex 2.
The Great Tornado Hunt (various)
Multiple TWC / Discovery specials.

Modern streaming

The Storm (Netflix)
Anthology of extreme weather events.
Twister: Nature's Fury (Netflix)
Historical event breakdowns.
Fastest, Deadliest, Strongest (Nat Geo)
Superlatives of natural forces.
Killer Storms (Nat Geo)
Case-by-case tornado analysis.
Storm Rising (Discovery+)
Chase-focused, event coverage.

Feature films (fictional but weather-accurate)

Twister (1996)
The original. Wildly inaccurate on some points but shaped the field.
Into the Storm (2014)
Fictional found-footage. Good VFX, hit-or-miss science.
Twisters (2024)
Sequel/reboot. Better meteorology than the original.
The Perfect Storm (2000)
Nor'easter, not tornado, but foundational storm cinema.
Sharknado (2013)
A joke, obviously. Mentioned for cultural completeness.

Short-form / YouTube

Ryan Hall Y'all recaps
Post-event storm analysis, millions of views.
Reed Timmer chase compilations
Extreme intercepts.
Pecos Hank videos
Photography-focused, poetic.
Skip Talbot case studies
Analytical, deep dives.
Max Velocity chase recaps
Faster-paced, event-focused.
Convective Chronicles
Historical outbreak deep dives.

Historical footage

Jarrell 1997 F5 footage
The famous "Dead Man Walking" tornado.
Andover 1991 F5 footage
Iconic wedge tornado.
Bridge Creek-Moore 1999 F5 footage
Various chasers.
Greensburg 2007 EF5 footage
Night tornado.
Joplin 2011 EF5 footage
Extensive citizen footage.
El Reno 2013 footage
Tim Samaras's last chase.

  • Netflix — modern anthology series.
  • Prime Video — feature films and older docs.
  • Nat Geo TV — superlatives-focused.
