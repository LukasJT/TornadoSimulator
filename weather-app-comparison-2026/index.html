AccuWeather deep dive

  • Longest US weather service.
  • MinuteCast: minute-by-minute precipitation forecast.
  • Wide international coverage.
  • Premium tier: ad removal.
  • Enterprise business heavily.
  • RealFeel temperature.
  • Storm names (their own branding).
  • Reasonably reliable.
  • Free tier ad-supported.

The Weather Channel deep dive

  • Owned by IBM (via Weather Company).
  • Video content strength.
  • National weather personalities.
  • Storm chase content.
  • Premium ad removal.
  • Consumer-oriented.
  • Solid alerts.
  • Some criticized for hype.

Windy deep dive

  • European (Czech).
  • Best visualization globally.
  • Multiple model options.
  • High-res radar.
  • Free tier excellent.
  • Premium unlocks HD radar.
  • Chaser-adjacent adoption.
  • Weather visualization gold standard.

RadarScope deep dive

  • US chaser standard.
  • Level 2/3 NEXRAD data.
  • Dual-pol layers.
  • Attribute overlays.
  • Pro tiers add power.
  • Not for casual users.
  • Companion to serious chase.
  • Windows/Mac/iOS/Android.

Apple Weather (Dark Sky) deep dive

  • Acquired from Dark Sky.
  • iOS default.
  • Minute precipitation forecast.
  • Elegant interface.
  • Watch integration.
  • iCloud integration.
  • US-focused originally, expanding.
  • No ads.
  • Free with iOS.

  • AI-augmented forecasting integrated.

The overall recommendation

Most people should have 2-3 apps:

  1. Apple Weather (if iOS) or Weather Channel (if Android) for daily.
  2. AccuWeather or Windy for detailed forecast.
  3. RadarScope for radar (if chase or spotter).
  4. NWS-specific for warnings (if in tornado country).
